## Quick facts

- **Location:** Khanom is in Nakhon Si Thammarat province on Thailand's Gulf coast. [Tourism Authority of Thailand](https://www.tourismthailand.org/Destinations/Provinces/Nakhon%20Si%20Thammarat/345)
- **Gateways:** Compare flights to Nakhon Si Thammarat and Surat Thani. Koh Samui is another option, but it adds a ferry and a road transfer.
- **Time and money:** Thailand uses ICT (UTC+7) and Thai baht. Carry some cash for small restaurants, drivers and boat operators.
- **Language:** Thai is the working language. Save your hotel name and destination in Thai for drivers outside the main resort strip.
- **Stay length:** Give Khanom three or four nights. Add separate nights for Cheow Lan Lake or Khao Sok rather than squeezing either into a beach stay.
- **Entry:** Visa rules depend on your passport. Check the current exemption rules before departure, and complete the official Thailand Digital Arrival Card within the required window before arrival. [Thailand Public Relations Department](https://thailand.prd.go.th/en/content/category/detail/id/48/iid/538547) [Thailand Digital Arrival Card](https://tdac.immigration.go.th/manual/en/faq.html)

## When to go

Khanom sits on the Gulf side of southern Thailand, so its weather pattern differs from the familiar Phuket-and-Krabi advice. The east coast of the south receives its heaviest seasonal rain during the northeast monsoon, with November commonly the wettest period. The Thai Meteorological Department notes that the southern east coast receives much of its rain in winter; treat every beach, cave and boat plan as weather dependent. [Thai Meteorological Department](https://tmd.go.th/info/%E0%B8%9B%E0%B8%A3%E0%B8%B4%E0%B8%A1%E0%B8%B2%E0%B8%93%E0%B8%9D%E0%B8%99)

| Month | What to expect |
|---|---|
| January | Rain usually begins to ease. Reserve the resort you want early if travelling around school holidays. |
| February | A good month for an unhurried beach stay and early-morning boat outings. |
| March | Hotter and often comparatively dry. Plan caves, cycling and long drives for the morning. |
| April | Hot weather and Songkran travel demand. Book transport before the holiday week. |
| May | Hot, with a mix of sunshine and showers. Choose a hotel with shade or a pool. |
| June | A quieter month for beach time with flexible day plans. |
| July | Keep a rain alternative for any boat outing. |
| August | Beach days remain possible, with more changeable conditions. |
| September | A useful value month if you can move activities around the forecast. |
| October | Rain becomes more frequent. Keep transfers and road conditions in mind. |
| November | The wettest part of the local calendar. Do not build a short visit around a single boat trip. |
| December | Monsoon rain can still affect Khanom even as much of Thailand enters its popular travel season. |

For the easiest first visit, start by comparing February and March. You still need a flexible plan, but these months usually give you a better chance of combining beach time with a boat trip. September can work well when room value matters more than guaranteed sunshine. In October and November, choose a hotel you would enjoy during a wet afternoon and avoid tight, non-refundable activity schedules.

Songkran falls on 13–15 April. For Loy Krathong, Sat Duan Sip and local festivals, check the current programme with the provincial tourism office before making a special trip; dates and venues change year to year. [Tourism Authority of Thailand](https://www.tourismthailand.org/Trip-Planner/Suggestion-Detail/recommended-route-in-nakhon-si-thammarat-for-2-days-1-night)

## Where to stay

Khanom is spread along beaches and small roads rather than built around a compact, walkable centre. Choose your hotel for the beach, restaurant access and transfer plan that suit your trip. Check the exact room rate, meal plan and cancellation terms for your dates instead of treating a destination average as a quote.

### Nadan Beach

Nadan is the practical place to begin if you want a long beach and a resort base. It suits first-time visitors who want the sea outside the hotel without committing to the quieter northern coast. Aava Resort & Spa is one established beachfront option; Talkoo Beach Resort is another property to compare.

The beach is your main nearby landmark. Some resorts have their own restaurant and pool, but the surrounding roads do not behave like a dense resort town where every meal and shop is a short walk away. Before booking, ask how far the room is from the beach, what is open after dark and how the hotel handles airport or pier transfers.

### Nai Phlao

Nai Phlao, also written Nai Plao, works well for a beach-and-resort stay beneath the green hills at the southern end of the main coast. Choose it when you want a quieter shoreline and are comfortable arranging transport for town errands and evening meals.

Hotel Villa Aokhanom Beachfront is on Nai Phlao Beach. The setting is the reason to stay here: sand, hills and a slower resort routine. Treat your hotel as the base rather than assuming town services are a short walk away. Confirm breakfast, restaurant hours and the cost of a return trip into Khanom before paying for a non-refundable room. [Tourism Authority of Thailand](https://www.tourismthailand.org/Attraction/thong-yee-bay)

### Khanom town

Town is a better fit when everyday services matter more than sleeping by the sea. You gain easier access to local food, shops and practical errands, while giving up the pleasure of walking straight from your room to the sand. This can be a sensible base for an independent traveller with a car or scooter and for anyone arriving late or leaving early.

Le Pes Villas describes a town location. Confirm the route from the minivan drop-off, the nearest meal options and your onward transport directly with the property. A map pin is more useful here than a broad promise of being “near Khanom,” because the district covers a wide area. [Le Pes Villas](https://www.lepesvillas.com/home)

### Thong Ching, Thong Yee and the north coast

The quieter northern coast suits travellers happy to spend most of the day at the beach, pool and resort. The Tourism Authority of Thailand describes Thong Yee Bay as a quiet bay beside Nai Phlao, reached by road from Khanom. Baan Thong Ching Resort is one beachfront option to compare, while the wider Thong Nian area shows how quickly this coast shifts from beach to mangrove and small local roads. [Tourism Authority of Thailand](https://www.tourismthailand.org/Attraction/thong-yee-bay)

Stay here for privacy and a slower day, not for spontaneous trips into town. Ask for the final access instructions, restaurant schedule and transfer price before choosing a remote room. If you plan a dawn boat outing, confirm whether pickup reaches your hotel or starts elsewhere in the district.

## Things to do

<GuidePhoto slot="activity" />

Khanom rewards a loose itinerary. Give beach days, boat trips and inland excursions enough room to move with the weather. Prices, pickup areas and activity availability should be confirmed with the operator before paying.

### Half-day outings

1. **Walk Nadan Beach.** Start early, when the sand is cooler and the light is softer. Swim only when conditions and local flags allow, and remember that a calm-looking morning can change quickly during the Gulf monsoon.

2. **Cycle the coastal roads.** A local operator advertises guided mountain-bike rides that combine coastal scenery, waterfalls and swimming stops. 3. **Visit Thong Yee Bay.** Drive the coastal route to the small bay south of Nai Phlao. It is a quiet stop for beach time, with fishing boats and rocky edges. [Tourism Authority of Thailand](https://www.tourismthailand.org/Attraction/thong-yee-bay)

### Full-day outings

4. **Take a pink-dolphin boat trip.** Leave room for a second attempt if dolphins are central to your visit: wildlife sightings and safe sea conditions cannot be guaranteed. Verify the boat, life jackets, cancellation policy and current pickup point before you <AffiliateLink type="klook" query="Khanom pink dolphin tour">compare pink-dolphin tours on Klook</AffiliateLink>.

5. **Explore Khao Wang Thong Cave.** The provincial tourism route includes Tham Khao Wang Thong alongside Khanom Beach, Ko Nui and Khao Hin Phap Pha. Use a local guide or driver, wear shoes with grip and confirm whether the cave is open after rain. [Tourism Authority of Thailand](https://www.tourismthailand.org/Trip-Planner/Suggestion-Detail/recommended-route-in-nakhon-si-thammarat-for-2-days-1-night)

6. **Pair the coast with an inland stop.** Hin Lat is a rocky stream and pool in Khlong Nai Phlao rather than a dramatic high waterfall. Keep the route flexible because recent rain determines water levels and road conditions. Ask locally before swimming and never enter fast or discoloured water.

7. **Drive toward Sichon.** Take a daylight coastal drive for beaches, food stops and a slower look at the Gulf coast. A rental car makes the most sense if you are comfortable driving in Thailand and hold the licence and insurance required for the vehicle.

## Getting around

Khanom is easier with a prearranged transfer, rental car or hotel driver than with an expectation of frequent local transport. Ask your hotel for the price and route from Nakhon Si Thammarat Airport, Surat Thani Airport or Donsak Pier before you book the room. From Koh Samui, compare the ferry timetable and onward road transfer as one journey, allowing enough time between the two.

The coast is longer and more spread out than it appears on a booking map. A hotel described as “Khanom” may be convenient for one beach and awkward for town, a cave or a boat meeting point. Save each destination pin offline and arrange the return leg before leaving a quiet bay. If you hire a driver for several stops, agree on waiting time and the final drop-off as well as the headline price.

Scooters give flexibility but are a poor shortcut if you lack the correct licence, experience or insurance. Inspect any rental before leaving, photograph existing damage and keep a copy of the rental agreement. Carry cash for small services and confirm card acceptance individually.

## Local tips + scams

<GuidePhoto slot="localTips" />

Respectful dress matters at temples and in private homes. Cover shoulders and knees when appropriate, keep your voice low around shrines and remove shoes where the host or temple asks. Avoid touching someone's head, do not point your feet at a Buddha image, and women should avoid direct physical contact with monks. [Responsible Thailand](https://responsiblethailand.com/thailand-travel-etiquette-essential-dos-and-donts/)

The practical risk in a spread-out beach district is an unclear transport or tour agreement. Confirm the full price, pickup point, waiting time, return arrangement and bad-weather policy in writing. For a wildlife trip, a reputable operator should never promise a dolphin sighting. For a rental, photograph the vehicle and do not leave your passport as collateral.

Use ATMs at banks or established businesses, cover your PIN and keep your card with you when paying. Carry smaller baht notes for local food, drivers and boat fees, then confirm whether a quoted amount is per person or per boat. [Government of Canada travel advice](https://travel.gc.ca/destinations/thailand)

Use only the official TDAC website linked in Quick facts. Third-party form services can look official while adding avoidable fees. Use mosquito protection, especially near still water, and avoid freshwater swimming after heavy rain. Check the current forecast and local advice before any boat, cave or stream outing.

## FAQ

### Is Khanom suitable for a solo female trip?

Yes, with a transport plan. Choose well-reviewed accommodation, arrange the first arrival before dark and avoid relying on a late ride from a remote beach. Share a boat or driver itinerary with someone you trust, keep normal drink and valuables precautions, and ask the hotel to organise unfamiliar transfers.

### How many days do I need?

Three or four nights works well for a beach day, a boat or wildlife outing and an inland excursion. The extra night gives you room to move a boat trip when the sea is rough. Add separate nights for Khao Sok or Cheow Lan Lake rather than treating either as a rushed day trip.

### Is Khanom expensive?

Your biggest variable is transport. A beach resort, private airport transfer and rental vehicle create a very different total from a town stay and local food. Price the room, arrival transfer and the activity you most want before setting a daily budget, then keep a reserve for a changed pickup or weather delay.

### Where should first-time visitors stay?

Start with Nadan Beach if you want a straightforward beach base with a wider choice of established resorts. Choose Nai Phlao when scenery and a quieter hotel routine matter more than town access. Book the northern coast only after you are comfortable with its meal and transport arrangements.

### Do I need cash?

Yes. Hotels and larger restaurants may accept cards, while small eateries, drivers and boat operators often work more easily in cash. Carry enough baht for the day without keeping your whole travel fund together, and ask whether card payments add a fee before handing over the card.

### Will I definitely see pink dolphins?

No. Boat operators can target the usual areas, but wildlife and sea conditions set the outcome. Keep the outing flexible, choose an operator that explains its safety and wildlife approach, and avoid treating a tour booking as a sighting guarantee.
