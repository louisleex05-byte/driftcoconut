## Quick facts

- **Currency:** Thai baht (฿ / THB). On 1 September 2026, the mid-market reference was about **US$1 = ฿33.22**, so ฿1,000 is roughly US$30.10. Bank-card, ATM and cash-exchange rates will differ. [pluang](https://pluang.com/en/tools/currency-converter/usd-thb)
- **Airport:** Most international visitors arrive through Bangkok’s **Suvarnabhumi Airport (BKK)**, then continue by road to Pattaya in around **1.5–2 hours**, depending on traffic. [thaiadvice](https://www.thaiadvice.com/en/pattaya/ulasim/bangkok-havalimanindan-pattayaya-nasil-gidilir)
- **Closer airport:** **U-Tapao–Rayong–Pattaya International Airport (UTP)** is nearer, though its route network is much smaller and schedules can shift. Check current flights before booking. **[UNCERTAIN]**
- **Time zone:** Indochina Time, **UTC+7**, with no daylight-saving time.
- **Language:** Thai is the main language. English is routine at hotels, malls, major attractions and tourist restaurants, but less reliable in local transport and small family-run businesses. Keep your hotel address saved in Thai.
- **Entry:** Thailand’s TDAC digital arrival form is mandatory for foreign arrivals. Visitors from the US, UK, Australia, Japan and many EU countries were listed for up to 30 days of visa-exempt entry from September 2026; this is visa exemption, not Visa on Arrival. Recheck requirements with an official Thai consular source before flying. [visitthailandtoday](https://www.visitthailandtoday.com/guides/thailand-visa-requirements) [nationthailand](https://www.nationthailand.com/news/tourism/40070511)
- **Useful daily budget:** Plan around **฿3,500–6,000 per person / US$105–181** for a mid-range day with a decent room, three meals and local transport. This is a practical planning range, not a fixed city price. **[UNCERTAIN]**

## When to go

Pattaya stays hot throughout the year. The choice is less about finding “winter” and more about deciding between reliable dry weather and lower room rates. November through February is the easiest period for beach days, Koh Larn ferries and long walks along Beach Road. June through October is wetter, cheaper, and better suited to travellers who do not mind arranging their day around rain radar.

<GuidePhoto slot="kohLarn" />

January is dry and warm, averaging a 31.0°C high, 22.0°C low and about 28 mm of rain. It is peak season, especially around New Year, so beachfront hotels and flights tend to cost more. February remains dry at about 31.4°C / 23.7°C with 42 mm of rain; it is one of the cleanest months for a Koh Larn day. [weatherjourney](https://weatherjourney.com/en/weather-pattaya-1614295)

March gets hotter, with an average high of 32.1°C and around 66 mm of rain. It is often a better-value shoulder-season choice than January or February, provided you do temple visits and viewpoint walks early. April is hotter again, around 32.8°C / 26.3°C, with roughly 93 mm of rain. Songkran falls on **13–15 April 2026** and **13–15 April 2027**; expect higher demand around the holiday and check Pattaya-specific water-festival arrangements close to the date. [weatherjourney](https://weatherjourney.com/en/weather-pattaya-1614295)

May begins the wet-season shift. Average rain rises to roughly 166 mm, followed by around 150 mm in June, 158 mm in July and 161 mm in August. Rain often arrives as a heavy afternoon burst rather than a full washed-out day, which means a morning ferry, museum or beach session can still work well. Hotel discounts become easier to find in this period. [weatherjourney](https://weatherjourney.com/en/weather-pattaya-1614295) [gotopattaya](https://www.gotopattaya.com/en/blog/pattaya-rainy-season)

September is one of the wettest months, with about 250 mm of rain and an average temperature around 30.7°C / 25.7°C. October remains wet, at approximately 224.5 mm, though conditions may begin easing later in the month. These are the months to build slack into island plans and avoid booking a tightly timed beach itinerary. [weatherjourney](https://weatherjourney.com/en/weather-pattaya-1614295) [realty-51](https://realty-51.com/en/blog/pattaya-climate-by-month)

November is the turn: rainfall drops to around 57 mm and hotel demand begins climbing. Pattaya International Fireworks Festival takes place on **27–28 November 2026**, with free public viewing along Pattaya Beach. December is the driest month in the research, with about 12 mm of rain, and one of the most expensive periods for hotels. [realty-51](https://realty-51.com/en/blog/pattaya-climate-by-month) [pattayaland](https://www.pattayaland.com/en/events/pattaya-international-fireworks) [weatherjourney](https://weatherjourney.com/en/weather-pattaya-1614295)

Pattaya is not in northern Thailand’s main burning-season zone, but Chonburi can still have poor PM2.5 days from haze, traffic, local burning and weather conditions. Check live air quality before a long outdoor day, especially if you have asthma or respiratory issues. [iqair](https://www.iqair.com/eu/air-quality/thailand/chon-buri/pattaya-city)

## Where to stay

### Central Pattaya: Beach Road and Second Road

This is Pattaya at its most convenient: the beach on one side, food, malls and songthaew routes on the other. You stay here when you want to walk out for breakfast, catch a baht bus without planning, and be close to Bali Hai Pier for Koh Larn.

It works well for first visits, short breaks and travellers who would rather not rent a motorbike. The trade-off is noise, traffic and a busier late-night atmosphere, especially around Walking Street and central Beach Road.

Typical mid-range rates run **฿1,800–4,500 per night / US$54–135**. A-One The Royal Cruise Hotel is often around **฿2,000–4,500 / US$60–135**, while Holiday Inn Pattaya is usually a more expensive option at approximately **฿3,500–7,000+ / US$105–211**. These are planning ranges, not guaranteed live rates. **[UNCERTAIN]**

From here, Pattaya Beach, Central Pattaya, Beach Road, Second Road, Terminal 21 Pattaya and much of the central dining scene are within reach on foot or by short songthaew ride. There is no BTS or MRT in Pattaya; use the Beach Road and Second Road songthaew loop, then continue to Bali Hai Pier for the island ferries. [thaiadvice](https://www.thaiadvice.com/en/pattaya/ulasim/bangkok-havalimanindan-pattayaya-nasil-gidilir)

### North Pattaya, Wong Amat and Naklua

North Pattaya feels more like a resort strip than central Pattaya, particularly around Wong Amat Beach. The roads are calmer, the beachfront hotels have more space, and you are away from the late-night pull of Walking Street without being far from restaurants and shopping.

Choose this area for a family stay, a couple’s trip, or a hotel-led holiday where pool time matters as much as going out. It also suits travellers who want better beach surroundings than central Pattaya without committing to Jomtien.

Expect mid-range rooms around **฿2,500–6,000 / US$75–181**. Amari Pattaya often falls around **฿3,500–7,000+ / US$105–211**, while Centara Grand Mirage Beach Resort Pattaya commonly sits higher, around **฿5,000–10,000+ / US$151–301**. Both are rate-sensitive resorts, so use those numbers as a booking target rather than a promise. **[UNCERTAIN]**

Wong Amat Beach, Terminal 21 Pattaya and north-side dining are nearby. The Sanctuary of Truth is a short ride away rather than a practical walk from most hotels. There is no rail station; North Pattaya Road connects easily to central Pattaya by songthaew, taxi or Bolt. ### Pratumnak Hill

Pratumnak is the quiet headland between central Pattaya and Jomtien. It is greener, lower-rise and more residential, with sea-view resorts, small cafés, Cozy Beach and access to the Big Buddha area.

It suits couples, longer-stay visitors and digital nomads who want a calmer base but still want central Pattaya within a short ride. You will spend more on rides here than in Central Pattaya because local songthaew coverage is less straightforward.

Mid-range rooms usually run **฿1,800–4,500 / US$54–135**, although many prominent properties are above that. InterContinental Pattaya Resort often prices around **฿4,500–9,000+ / US$135–271**; The Monttra Pattaya is typically in the **฿4,000–8,000+ / US$120–241** range. **[UNCERTAIN]**

Cozy Beach, the Khao Phra Bat viewpoint, Wat Phra Yai and the Big Buddha are the main nearby landmarks. There is no BTS, MRT or rail stop. Bolt and taxis are the simplest option, particularly after dark.

### Jomtien

Jomtien runs south from Pattaya’s core along a longer beach road with more breathing room. It is less central, but it has a local-residential rhythm, beach restaurants, cafés and generally better value for longer stays.

Stay here if you want morning walks by the water, room to work from a hotel or condo, and less nightlife outside your door. Families often find it easier than Central Pattaya, while budget-conscious beach travellers can get more space for the money.

A typical mid-range room costs **฿1,200–3,500 / US$36–105**. D Varee Jomtien Beach commonly appears around **฿1,500–3,500 / US$45–105**, while Marine Beach Hotel Pattaya is often around **฿2,000–4,500 / US$60–135**. **[UNCERTAIN]**

Jomtien Beach, beach-road food stalls, casual restaurants and watersports are within walking distance. Pattaya has no urban rail network, so use beach-road songthaews and ride-hailing to reach Central Pattaya; allow extra time during rain or evening traffic. An official taxi costs around **฿1,400–1,700 / US$42–51** and makes more sense if you arrive late, have children, or are carrying beach luggage and camera equipment. ## Local tips + scams

Respect lands quickly in Thailand. At temples and private homes, remove your shoes when expected and dress conservatively at religious sites. Cover your shoulders and knees before entering. Do not point your feet at people, Buddha images or temple objects, and never touch someone’s head.

Your tone matters too. Do not shout at a driver, cashier or restaurant worker when something goes wrong. Stay calm, state the problem clearly, and decline an offer with “mai ao khrap” if you are male or “mai ao kha” if you are female: it means “no thank you” and will take you further than an argument.

The jet-ski damage scam is the one to take seriously on Pattaya Beach. Some operators have been reported to claim that pre-existing marks are new damage and demand payment. The safest option is to skip informal beach rentals; if you do rent, record a continuous video of the hull and panels first, do not hand over your passport, and call Tourist Police if pressured. [pattayaexcursions](https://pattayaexcursions.com/guide/pattaya-scams-and-safety)

Be cautious with parked taxis and transport touts offering a “special price.” Use the official airport queue, Bolt, or agree the fare in advance when a meter is not being used. [pattayaexcursions](https://pattayaexcursions.com/guide/pattaya-scams-and-safety)

Also avoid following street touts into bars or venues. Bar-bill padding and disputed checks are still reported: keep track of what you order, ask for an itemised bill, and settle questions before paying. [pattayaexcursions](https://pattayaexcursions.com/guide/pattaya-scams-and-safety)

Save **Tourist Police 1155**, general emergency **191**, and ambulance **1669** in your phone. [pattayaexcursions](https://pattayaexcursions.com/guide/pattaya-scams-and-safety)

One local habit worth learning: use the Beach Road–Second Road songthaew loop as a moving connection, not a door-to-door taxi. Ride the vehicle’s existing route for the normal ฿10–20 fare; directing the driver converts it into a negotiated charter. [thaiadvice](https://www.thaiadvice.com/en/pattaya/ulasim/bangkok-havalimanindan-pattayaya-nasil-gidilir)

## FAQ

### Is Pattaya safe for solo female travellers?

Yes, with normal city and nightlife awareness. Use reviewed hotels, book rides through apps after dark, keep control of drinks, and avoid isolated beach areas late at night. The larger practical risks are road traffic, transport disputes and nightlife scams rather than violent crime against tourists. [pattayaexcursions](https://pattayaexcursions.com/guide/pattaya-scams-and-safety)

### How many days do I need?

Plan three to four nights for a first visit. That gives you time for a beach-and-city day, a Koh Larn ferry day, one cultural or family attraction, and enough room for rain or arrival delays. Add a night if you are coming for the November fireworks weekend. **[UNCERTAIN]**

### Is Pattaya expensive?

It can be, but it does not need to be. Backpacker-level spending can sit around **฿1,200–2,500 / US$36–75** daily; mid-range travel is around **฿3,500–6,000 / US$105–181**; luxury stays begin around **฿8,000 / US$241** per day. These are planning ranges. **[UNCERTAIN]**

### What is the best area for first-timers?

Central Pattaya near Beach Road or Second Road is the easiest first base. You can walk to the beach, malls, food and much of the city’s activity, then use the songthaew loop and reach Bali Hai Pier without arranging every ride. It is convenient, not quiet. [thaiadvice](https://www.thaiadvice.com/en/pattaya/ulasim/bangkok-havalimanindan-pattayaya-nasil-gidilir)

### Do I need cash in Pattaya?

Bring both cash and cards. Hotels, malls and larger restaurants generally take cards, while songthaews, markets, street food and small vendors often work best with Thai baht. When an ATM offers conversion to your home currency, decline it and choose Thai baht to avoid dynamic currency conversion. [destinationthailand](https://destinationthailand.io/thailand-scams/)

### Is Koh Larn worth doing from Pattaya?

Yes, if you leave early and keep expectations realistic. It is the simplest way to swap central Pattaya’s urban beach for clearer water and a proper island day. Go on a weekday where possible, check sea conditions in wet months, and keep your return timing flexible if storms build. **[UNCERTAIN]**
