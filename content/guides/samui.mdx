## Quick facts

- **Airport:** Samui International Airport (USM), a short drive from Chaweng and Bophut.
- **Currency:** Thai baht (THB / ฿). This guide uses ฿33 ≈ US$1 for easy planning; check the live rate before you exchange or book.
- **Time:** Indochina Time (ICT), UTC+7. Thailand does not use daylight saving time.
- **Language:** Thai is the working language. English is common in hotels, tour desks and established restaurants, but less reliable once you leave the main beach roads.
- **A mid-range day:** Budget around ฿4,000–6,500 (US$121–197) per person when sharing a mid-range hotel room, eating three meals out, and using local transport. Mid-range rooms commonly sit around ฿2,500–4,500 (US$76–136), depending on season. [travelguidestip](https://travelguidestip.com/koh-samui-hotels/)
- **Visa note for September 2026:** From 15 September 2026, visitors from the US, UK, Australia, Japan and many European countries are reported to receive 30-day visa-exempt entry, rather than a visa on arrival. Check your exact passport rules with an official Thai embassy before flying; South Korea’s arrangement requires particular care because reporting around the 2026 rule change is inconsistent. [legit](https://www.legit.ng/world/asia/1728367-thailand-cuts-visa-free-stay-60-30-days-60-countries-announces-commencement-date/)
- **The weather pattern:** Samui’s wettest stretch is usually October to December, unlike Phuket and Krabi on the Andaman side. February to April is the clearest dry-season bet; July and August are a useful second beach window. ## When to go

Samui runs on its own weather calendar. It stays warm all year, but its heaviest rain tends to arrive late in the year, when much of the Andaman coast is drying out. For beach time, February through April is the safest stretch. If you are tied to northern-hemisphere summer holidays, July and August can work well too, with more passing showers and busier family resorts. [mayaresortsamui](https://mayaresortsamui.com/family-journal/koh-samui-weather-practical-seasonal-guide-for-families/)

January sits at 24.1–29°C, with about 137.8 mm of rain across roughly 11 rainy days. The weather improves through the month, but post-New Year hotel rates can still be high. February is Samui’s driest month in the cited climate data: 25–29.5°C, around 57.8 mm of rain, and only 5.5 rainy days. That makes it prime boat-trip weather—and prime hotel-pricing weather. [mayaresortsamui](https://mayaresortsamui.com/family-journal/koh-samui-weather-practical-seasonal-guide-for-families/) March remains dry at 25.6–30.7°C and roughly 77.8 mm of rain, although the afternoons become properly hot. [mayaresortsamui](https://mayaresortsamui.com/family-journal/koh-samui-weather-practical-seasonal-guide-for-families/)

April reaches 32.1°C and stays relatively dry, but Songkran changes the rhythm. Thailand’s New Year water festival runs from **13–15 April 2026** and **13–15 April 2027**. Expect busy flights, fuller beachfront hotels and a lot of water flying around Chaweng and Lamai. [thaiholidayguide](https://www.thaiholidayguide.com/events/events-calendar/) It is a good time to travel if you want the celebration; it is not the week to expect a quiet poolside break.

May brings 146.5 mm of rain and short, humid showers. June is a useful value month at 25.5–32.2°C, with about 112.7 mm of rain. July and August stay hot—roughly 25–32°C—with rainfall around 119–123 mm. Families from Europe push up demand in August, particularly around beach resorts, but you can still find better value than February. [mayaresortsamui](https://mayaresortsamui.com/family-journal/koh-samui-weather-practical-seasonal-guide-for-families/) [baanlyy](https://baanlyy.com/thailand/koh-samui/weather)

September is shoulder season: 24.8–31.6°C, 116.8 mm of rainfall and more frequent showers later in the month. It is a decent time if your schedule can flex around the sea forecast. October changes the conversation, with rainfall climbing to 290.2 mm. November is the rainiest month in the cited data at 489.6 mm and around 19.6 rainy days; room rates can soften, but ferry and boat-trip disruption becomes more likely. [mayaresortsamui](https://mayaresortsamui.com/family-journal/koh-samui-weather-practical-seasonal-guide-for-families/) December remains wet at about 209.1 mm, then hotel rates climb sharply around Christmas and New Year. [baanlyy](https://baanlyy.com/thailand/koh-samui/weather)

Samui is not northern Thailand’s burning-season problem area, but occasional regional haze can still happen. Check live AQI before committing to a hiking or cycling day. Mosquitoes deserve more attention than smog here, particularly after rain; dengue occurs year-round in Thailand. [visitthailandtoday](https://www.visitthailandtoday.com/guides/is-thailand-safe)

## Where to stay

### Chaweng

Chaweng is the island’s practical, energetic base: long beach, late dinners, shopping, bars and the widest selection of hotels all gathered along the east coast. It suits first-timers who want to walk to dinner, solo travelers who do not want to feel isolated after dark, and anyone who wants the option of a lively night without arranging a car home. [visitthailandtoday](https://www.visitthailandtoday.com/guides/where-to-stay-in-koh-samui)

A typical mid-range room runs around ฿2,000–4,500 (US$61–136) a night, though Christmas, New Year and February dates can be higher. Chaweng Cove Beach Resort and OZO Chaweng Samui are useful names to compare when you are pricing beach access against room size and pool facilities. Their nightly rates move with the booking date, so use live availability rather than treating any one listed price as fixed. [travelguidestip](https://travelguidestip.com/koh-samui-hotels/)

From the central strip, you can walk to Chaweng Beach, Central Samui, beach bars, pharmacies, casual Thai restaurants and late-night food. Samui has no BTS, MRT or rail station; your nearest useful transport is the ring-road songthaew route or a pre-arranged taxi. The airport is usually about 10–15 minutes away. ### Bophut and Fisherman’s Village

Bophut is for evenings that begin with a sea breeze and end over dinner, rather than at a club. It works well for couples, food-focused travelers, boutique-hotel stays and first-timers who would rather walk home along the waterfront than through Chaweng’s louder central strip. ### Lamai

Lamai keeps the useful parts of a beach town—restaurants, markets, massage shops, bars and tour desks—without matching Chaweng’s pace. It is a solid choice for families, couples and longer stays where you want restaurants nearby but do not need a nightlife strip outside the door. Mid-range rooms generally fall around ฿1,500–3,500 (US$45–106) per night. Lamai Wanta Beach Resort and Thai House Beach Resort are both worth checking when comparing dates. The lower starting rate compared with Chaweng means Lamai can leave more room in the budget for boat days, private drivers or a better room category. ### Maenam

Maenam has the slower beach rhythm many visitors expect from Samui before they arrive in Chaweng. Its long north-coast beach, smaller-scale dining and lower room prices make it a comfortable base for digital nomads, couples staying a little longer and travelers who are happy to trade nightlife for a quieter evening. [visitthailandtoday](https://www.visitthailandtoday.com/guides/where-to-stay-in-koh-samui)

A mid-range room often lands around ฿1,000–2,500 (US$30–76) per night. Escape Beach Resort and Saree Samui are two places to compare, depending on whether you prioritize a resort setting or a closer village feel. These rates remain date-sensitive, especially in the dry-season peak. [visitthailandtoday](https://www.visitthailandtoday.com/guides/where-to-stay-in-koh-samui)

Walkability depends on the exact hotel: you can reach Maenam Beach, small cafés and local eateries from the central beach-road pockets, but this is not a continuous nightlife strip. There is no rail station. Use songthaews during the day and a taxi or driver when you are returning late. The airport is about 20 minutes away. ## Things to do

### Half-day

**Big Buddha and Wat Plai Laem** make an easy north-coast morning or late-afternoon circuit. Both are free to enter, though donations are welcome. Cover your shoulders and knees, take off your shoes where asked, and do not arrive in swimwear straight from the beach. [visitthailandtoday](https://www.visitthailandtoday.com/guides/things-to-do-in-koh-samui)

<GuidePhoto slot="bigBuddha" />

**Fisherman’s Village Friday Walking Street** is best treated as dinner rather than a rushed shopping stop. Entry is free; your spend depends on food, drinks and whatever you pick up from the stalls. Go early enough to eat before the waterfront gets crowded. [visitthailandtoday](https://www.visitthailandtoday.com/guides/things-to-do-in-koh-samui)

### Full-day

**Ang Thong Marine National Park** is the big Samui boat day: a 42-island archipelago with viewpoints, Emerald Lake, snorkeling, kayaking and lunch on most itineraries. A slow boat is generally ฿1,200–2,000 (US$36–61); speedboats commonly run ฿1,800–2,800 (US$55–85). Check whether the ฿300 (US$9) adult park fee is included before you pay. ## Getting around

Samui has no BTS, MRT, metro or train line. Your everyday choices are songthaews, taxis, private drivers, rental cars and scooters. The red shared pickup trucks are the useful budget option on the ring road: expect about ฿30–80 (US$0.90–2.40) in daytime, and roughly ฿100–200 (US$3–6) for longer or later rides. ## Local tips + scams

Temple manners start with clothing. A bikini is fine on the sand; it is not appropriate at Big Buddha, Wat Plai Laem, donation places or hospitals. Bring a shirt or wrap in your day bag, cover your shoulders and knees, and remove shoes when signs or staff ask you to.

<GuidePhoto slot="bigBuddha" />

The head and feet matter in Thai social etiquette. Do not touch someone’s head, even playfully. Avoid pointing the soles of your feet at people, Buddha images, shrine areas or monks. Keep them low when you sit in a temple.

Treat royal and religious subjects carefully. Thailand’s laws around criticism of the monarchy are serious, and Buddhist images, monks and temple spaces are not props for disrespectful jokes or photos. Keep public conversations measured, especially when alcohol is involved. [visitthailandtoday](https://www.visitthailandtoday.com/guides/is-thailand-safe)

Three money-saving habits will save you trouble. First, photograph and video every side of a scooter or jet ski before accepting it; disputed damage claims can turn into expensive cash demands. Second, agree taxi fares before leaving, because Samui does not have a dependable metered-taxi system. Third, read boat-tour inclusions closely: a low price can exclude transfers, kayaking, insurance or Ang Thong’s ฿300 park fee. [visitthailandtoday](https://www.visitthailandtoday.com/guides/is-thailand-safe) [outthailand](https://outthailand.com/guide/ang-thong-marine-park/)

My practical local tip: use songthaews when the sun is up and the route works for you; do not assume you will find the same service after dinner. For a return from a remote beach, hillside restaurant or late-night venue, sort a driver or taxi before you sit down to eat. [outthailand](https://outthailand.com/guide/getting-around-koh-samui/)

## FAQ

### Is Samui safe for solo female travelers?

Samui is generally manageable for solo female travelers who use the same care they would on any resort island. Stay in a well-reviewed property, arrange transport before late-night drinks, avoid isolated beach roads after dark, and do not take a scooter unless you are licensed and confident. Road accidents are a more immediate concern than violent crime. [visitthailandtoday](https://www.visitthailandtoday.com/guides/is-thailand-safe)

### How many days do I need in Samui?

Plan four or five nights for a first trip. That gives you one easy arrival day, one beach day, one Ang Thong day, one island-road day and one flexible buffer for weather. If you are adding Koh Phangan or Koh Tao, give the wider Gulf trip at least seven to nine nights.

### Is Samui expensive?

It can be, especially between late December and March. A backpacker can work with about ฿1,200–2,500 (US$36–76) daily, while a comfortable mid-range trip usually sits near ฿4,000–6,500 (US$121–197) per person. Hostels are reported from around ฿350, street food from about ฿80, and songthaews from roughly ฿50. [travelguidestip](https://travelguidestip.com/koh-samui-on-a-budget/)

### What is the best area for first-timers?

Choose Chaweng if you want the easiest first visit: a central beach, restaurants, shops, nightlife and plenty of hotel choice. Pick Bophut instead if you want waterfront dinners and a calmer evening base. Both are close to the airport, while Bophut feels less concentrated around nightlife. [visitthailandtoday](https://www.visitthailandtoday.com/guides/where-to-stay-in-koh-samui)

### Do I need cash in Samui?

Yes. Hotels, larger restaurants and established tours often take cards, but songthaews, market stalls, small food shops and incidental tour fees frequently work better in cash. Carry smaller baht notes for rides and meals. Do not assume that an advertised tour price includes the Ang Thong national-park fee. [visitthailandtoday](https://www.visitthailandtoday.com/guides/things-to-do-in-koh-samui)

### Can I rely on the weather forecast for an Ang Thong trip?

Use it as a guide, not a guarantee. February through April usually offers the strongest odds for calm sea conditions, while October through December brings Samui’s wetter monsoon period and more potential disruption. Book a tour with a clear cancellation policy, then keep one alternate island day in your itinerary. [mayaresortsamui](https://mayaresortsamui.com/family-journal/koh-samui-weather-practical-seasonal-guide-for-families/)
