## Quick facts

- Airport: Nan Nakhon, code NNT; central Nan is approximately 10–15 minutes away by road. [kupi](https://www.kupi.com/en/explore/thailand/nan/nan-airport)
- Money: Thai baht. Conversions below use approximately THB 33.57 = US$1; your actual exchange rate will differ. [investing](https://www.investing.com/currencies/usd-thb-historical-data)
- Time: UTC+7, without daylight saving. [timeanddate](https://www.timeanddate.com/time/zone/@1608452)
- Language: Thai is your practical default; don’t assume English outside tourism-facing businesses. [tourismthailand](https://www.tourismthailand.org/Articles/plan-your-trip-language-and-culture)
- Daily budget: allow roughly THB 1,510–2,180 / US$45–65 per person sharing a mid-range room, excluding flights and major private excursions. This is a planning estimate, not a fixed local tariff. [kupi](https://www.kupi.com/en/explore/thailand/nan/nan-airport)
- Entry: from 15 September 2026, US, UK, EU, Australian and Japanese ordinary-passport holders generally receive 30-day visa-exempt tourist entry—not Visa on Arrival. Check your nationality’s current conditions before departure. [tatnews](https://www.tatnews.org/2026/09/thailand-introduces-new-30-day-and-15-day-visa-exemption-rules-from-15-september/)
- Rail: Nan has no passenger railway station or metro. [ticket2attraction](https://www.ticket2attraction.com/blogs/best-places-to-visit-nan/)

## When to go

Choose **November–January** for your first trip: cooler nights, less rain and generally more comfortable sightseeing. Treat the season labels below as planning guidance, not measured hotel-occupancy rankings. Temperatures are rounded city averages; mountain conditions differ. [weatherspark](https://weatherspark.com/y/113587/Average-Weather-in-Nan-Thailand-Year-Round)

- January, 14–29°C: very dry and a sensible peak-season choice.
- February, 17–32°C: still dry, but warming; make outdoor plans conditional on air quality.
- March, 20–35°C: mostly dry, increasingly hot, with regional haze a concern.
- April, 23–36°C: the hottest stretch, with thunderstorms increasing. [weatherspark](https://weatherspark.com/y/149089/Average-Weather-at-Nan-Thailand-Year-Round)
- May, 24–34°C: frequent showers mark the monsoon transition.
- June, 25–32°C: humid, regularly wet and better suited to flexible plans.
- July, 24–32°C: frequent rain and cloud.
- August, 24–31°C: typically the wettest month.
- September, 24–32°C: still wet; don’t assume mountain parks have reopened.
- October, 22–31°C: a shoulder-season transition, with rain still possible.
- November, 19–30°C: rainfall drops and cooler nights return.
- December, 16–29°C: dry, with cool mornings and evenings. [weatherspark](https://weatherspark.com/y/149089/Average-Weather-at-Nan-Thailand-Year-Round)

Think of May–September as the flexible, wet-season window rather than a guaranteed bargain. Compare actual hotel dates: there isn’t evidence for a Nan-wide percentage discount, and festivals can interrupt quieter periods. [en.kayak.co](https://www.en.kayak.co.th/Nan-Hotels-Nan-Seasons-Boutique-Resort.2182753.ksp)

### Historic core: Wat Phumin–Khuang Muang–Sumon Thewarat Road

You’re closest to the main heritage sights here, making this my first choice when you don’t want a vehicle. Depending on your hotel, you can walk between Wat Phumin, Nan National Museum, Wat Phra That Chang Kham and the central market area. Neither has a verified September quote here. Nan Bus Terminal is your onward transport anchor—not a metro station. ## Things to do

### Half-day

1. Wat Phumin and Nan National Museum. Start with the temple murals, then use the former palace museum to put the city’s history in context. Wat Phumin is free; published sources disagree between THB 100 and THB 120 for foreign-adult museum admission, so confirm the current price before visiting. Aim for Wednesday–Sunday, 09:00–16:00. [sawadiscovery](https://www.sawadiscovery.com/guide-thailande/attraction/wat-phumin)

<GuidePhoto slot="phuminMural" />

2. Wat Phra That Khao Noi at sunrise. Head uphill for the Buddha viewpoint, then return to town for breakfast. Temple entry is reported as free; transport and breakfast are extra, so arrange your return ride before leaving town. [offpaththailand](https://offpaththailand.com/things-to-do-in-nan-thailand/)

### Full-day

3. Pua valley and Wat Phuket. Give yourself a day for the valley and rice-field scenery rather than rushing out for one photograph. Late afternoon suits the viewpoint. No current admission charge was verified; budget separately for transport, meals and any workshops. [offpaththailand](https://offpaththailand.com/things-to-do-in-nan-thailand/)

<GuidePhoto slot="puaCountryside" />

4. Nan Riverside Arts Space. Pair contemporary art with an unhurried rural lunch; the full-day allocation includes travel and eating, not eight hours inside the gallery. One secondary source lists admission at THB 20 / US$0.60, but confirm the current fee and opening days directly; transport costs extra. [tourismthailand](https://www.tourismthailand.org/Attraction/nan-riverside-art-gallery)

5. Doi Phu Kha. Explore highland viewpoints and choose a walk that matches conditions, checking trails with park staff first. Foreign-adult admission is THB 200 / US$6, plus THB 30 / US$0.90 for a car; the published annual closure is 1 June–30 September, so confirm reopening before travelling. [thainationalparks](https://www.thainationalparks.com/doi-phu-kha-national-park)

<GuidePhoto slot="doiPhuKha" />

6. Sao Din Na Noi and Si Nan viewpoints. Combine the eroded earth formations with southern Nan’s mountain scenery, keeping an early start for viewpoints. Si Nan admission is THB 100 / US$3 per foreign adult; do not assume that fee covers every stop on your route, and budget separately for transport. [ticket2attraction](https://www.ticket2attraction.com/blogs/best-places-to-visit-nan/)

<GuidePhoto slot="saoDinNaNoi" />

### Multi-day excursions

7. Pua–Bo Kluea–Sapan, two or three days. Leave time for valley scenery, inland salt production and Sapan rather than turning the mountain road into a checklist. No verified package price is available: budget separately for lodging and transport, adding THB 200 / US$6 if entering Doi Phu Kha, and drive in daylight. [ticket2attraction](https://www.ticket2attraction.com/blogs/best-places-to-visit-nan/)

<GuidePhoto slot="activity" />

8. Doi Samer Dao overnight. Stay for sunset, stars and sunrise rather than making another long return drive. Foreign-adult park entry is THB 100 / US$3. A secondary guide reports an own-tent pitching fee of THB 30 / US$0.90; confirm the fee, camping dates, equipment and reservations directly, since published operating seasons differ. [tourismthailand](https://www.tourismthailand.org/Attraction/si-nan-national-park)

## Local tips + scams

At Wat Phumin, take your shoes off before entering the worship building and cover shoulders and knees. You don’t necessarily need to remove shoes across every outdoor courtyard; watch the entrance and follow the signs. [sawadiscovery](https://www.sawadiscovery.com/guide-thailande/attraction/wat-phumin)

When you sit, tuck your feet away rather than pointing your soles at a Buddha image or another person. Keep your hands away from people’s heads, including children’s, and don’t pass objects over them. These small adjustments matter more than attempting an elaborate greeting. [thailandfoundation.or](https://thailandfoundation.or.th/feet-low-head-high-a-guide-to-thai-etiquettes/)

For scams, keep the geography honest. The following are documented Thailand-wide risks, not proof of three active Nan operations:

- Fake TDAC websites: the official arrival-card process is free. Don’t mistake a paid lookalike for a compulsory government charge. [khaosodenglish](https://www.khaosodenglish.com/tourism/2026/03/29/10-of-foreign-arrivals-used-fake-tdac-sites-immigration-warns/)
- Rental damage disputes: avoid leaving your passport as security, and photograph the vehicle before you ride away. Passport retention can make a disagreement much harder to resolve. [gov](https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/thailand/safety-and-security)
- ATM skimming and card fraud: favour bank-located machines, shield your PIN and keep your card in sight during payment. [gov](https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/thailand/safety-and-security)

There’s no reason to import another destination’s scam reputation into Nan. Equally, a quieter trip doesn’t remove the need for ordinary precautions.

One useful detail: the museum’s frangipani arch is outside the exhibition building and is reported as accessible without buying museum admission. Make it a separate photo stop if that’s all you want, but check grounds access first—especially when the museum itself is closed—because access can change. [thailandaddict](https://thailandaddict.com/en/nan-national-museum)

## FAQ

### Is Nan safe for solo female travellers?

Choose a central hotel and arrange your evening transport rather than relying on an unsolicited lift. There’s no Nan-specific dataset supporting “completely safe.” Standard precautions still apply: avoid isolated areas alone after dark, watch your drink and use licensed transport. The walkable historic core reduces unnecessary journeys. [vietnamparadisetravel](https://www.vietnamparadisetravel.com/travel-guide/nan)

### How many days do I need?

Give yourself two nights for the city and nearby viewpoints. Four or five days makes more sense when you add Pua, Bo Kluea and Sapan. These are suggested itinerary lengths, not average visitor stays; add time again if you want a separate southern camping excursion. [vietnamparadisetravel](https://www.vietnamparadisetravel.com/travel-guide/nan)

### Is Nan expensive?

Plan around THB 1,510–2,180 / US$45–65 daily when sharing a mid-range room, or THB 2,010–3,020 / US$60–90 with a room to yourself. Those are constructed allowances covering meals and modest local transport. Private drivers, flights and substantial excursions sit outside them and need separate quotations. [kupi](https://www.kupi.com/en/explore/thailand/nan/nan-airport)

### What’s the best area for first-timers?

Stay around Wat Phumin, Khuang Muang and Sumon Thewarat Road if you want the simplest introduction. You can reach the principal heritage sights on foot from suitable hotels. Check the exact property location: “Nan” also covers resort addresses that require a car or arranged transfer. [en.kayak.co](https://www.en.kayak.co.th/Nan-Hotels-Nan-Seasons-Boutique-Resort.2182753.ksp)

### Do I need cash?

Yes—carry cash for small food vendors and informal transport rather than assuming your card works everywhere. A displayed Thai QR code doesn’t guarantee compatibility with your overseas banking app. Ask your hotel about accepted cards, deposits and payment conditions before arrival, particularly when booking through another platform. [offpaththailand](https://offpaththailand.com/thailand-street-food-prices-2026/)

### Can I visit Doi Phu Kha in September?

Don’t build a September itinerary around guaranteed park access. The published annual closure runs from 1 June through 30 September, and individual routes still need confirmation when the park reopens. You can visit Nan province without entering the park; keep your mountain plans conditional on official access and weather. [thainationalparks](https://www.thainationalparks.com/doi-phu-kha-national-park)
