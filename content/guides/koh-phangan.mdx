## Quick facts

- **Airport:** Koh Phangan has no commercial airport. Fly into Koh Samui Airport (USM), then continue by ferry; Surat Thani International Airport (URT) is the lower-cost mainland alternative. [Trip.com](https://www.trip.com/transport/nearby-airports/koh-phangan.html)
- **Currency:** Thai baht (THB / ฿). At publication, plan around ฿33.42 to US$1; card and ATM rates will differ. [XE](https://www.xe.com/en-us/currencyconverter/convert/?Amount=1&From=USD&To=THB)
- **Time:** Indochina Time, UTC+7, with no daylight saving time.
- **Language:** Thai is the local language. English is widely used at resorts, dive shops, cafés, and tour counters.
- **Visa:** From 15 September 2026, US, UK, EU, Australian, and Japanese passport holders can enter Thailand visa-free for tourism for up to 30 days, subject to immigration conditions. This is not Visa on Arrival. [TAT News](https://www.tatnews.org/2026/09/thailand-introduces-new-30-day-and-15-day-visa-exemption-rules-from-15-september/)
- **Daily mid-range budget:** Around ฿4,000–6,000 per person, or US$120–180, including a shared mid-range room, local meals, and some transport. [Nomadic Matt](https://www.nomadicmatt.com/travel-guides/thailand-travel-tips/ko-phangan/)
- **Best first base:** Stay in Thong Sala for ferry convenience and food, or Sri Thanu for beach cafés and slower west-coast evenings. [Out Thailand](https://outthailand.com/guide/where-to-stay-koh-phangan/)
- **Weather note:** Koh Phangan avoids northern Thailand’s severe smoke-season problem; your real weather concern is heavy rain, rough seas, and ferry disruption in October and November. [Tathago](https://tathago.co/weather/koh-phangan/)

## When to go

For clear water, reliable boat days, and long beach afternoons, come between February and April. Koh Phangan is warm all year, but its rainfall pattern is different from Phuket and Thailand’s Andaman coast: the wettest stretch arrives late, with October and November carrying the biggest downpours. [Tathago](https://tathago.co/weather/koh-phangan/)

January is warm, around 26°C by day and 24°C at night, with 76–85 mm of rainfall. The monsoon is easing, but New Year demand remains high, especially near the beach and around Full Moon Party dates. February is the island’s easy answer for a classic beach holiday: roughly 27°C by day, 24°C at night, and only 40–56 mm of rain. Hotels are busy and rates are high, particularly in Sri Thanu, Haad Yao, and Haad Rin. [Tathago](https://tathago.co/weather/koh-phangan/)

<GuidePhoto slot="fullMoonParty" />

March stays hot and dry, near 28°C during the day, with rainfall around 60 mm. It is one of the strongest months for snorkeling, diving, and Ang Thong Marine Park trips. April is hotter again, often around 32°C in the afternoon, with occasional storms and roughly 80–82 mm of rain. Songkran falls from 13–15 April 2026, bringing domestic holiday traffic and a little more demand for rooms and ferries. [Tathago](https://tathago.co/weather/koh-phangan/)

May through August is the value window. You will still get beach weather, but showers become more frequent: around 137–150 mm in May, 113–130 mm in June, 140–155 mm in July, and 113–130 mm in August. July and August can become busy with European school holidays, while Haad Rin prices climb around each Full Moon Party. [Tathago](https://tathago.co/weather/koh-phangan/)

September becomes cloudier, with around 180–187 mm of rain. October is wetter still, at roughly 263–290 mm, and November is the island’s wettest month, with 305–380 mm. This is when you can find lower hotel prices, but do not lock yourself into non-refundable ferries or boat excursions. December begins to improve, though rain can still arrive; Christmas and New Year push prices sharply upward. [Tathago](https://tathago.co/weather/koh-phangan/)

The remaining 2026 Full Moon Party dates are 26 September, 27 October, 24 November, 24 December, and 31 December. In 2027, dates currently listed include 22 January, 21 February, 22 March, 21 April, 20 May, 19 June, 18 July, 17 August, 15 September, and 16 October. The 21 February 2027 party is shifted because 22 February is Makha Bucha Day; the October 2026 party moved to 27 October because 26 October is the end of Buddhist Lent. [Full Moon Party Thailand](https://www.fullmoonpartythailand.com/dates)

## Where to stay

### Thong Sala

Thong Sala is Koh Phangan’s practical center: a ferry town with market food, pharmacies, ATMs, coffee, scooter rentals, and the island’s main transport connections. It suits first-timers, short stays, travelers arriving late by ferry, and anyone who wants to explore more than one coast without committing to a remote beach.

Mid-range rooms generally sit around ฿1,000–2,800, or US$30–84, though actual rates rise during Christmas, New Year, and Full Moon Party weeks. [Out Thailand](https://outthailand.com/guide/where-to-stay-koh-phangan/) The research does not provide two reliable current Thong Sala hotel rate snapshots, so use live booking results rather than publishing fixed hotel pricing for this area.

You can walk to Thong Sala Pier, Pantip Market, food stalls, ferry offices, convenience stores, cafés, and the town’s everyday services. It is not the island’s prettiest beach base, but it works well when you want breakfast near the pier, a simple lunch at the market, and a short taxi ride out to the west coast.

Thong Sala Pier is the nearest and most useful transport hub. Private taxis from the pier are commonly quoted around ฿300–500, or US$9–15; confirm the full fare before you get in. [Phanganist](https://phanganist.com/koh-phangan/thong-sala-pier)

### Sri Thanu and Haad Chao Phao

Sri Thanu is the west-coast base for yoga studios, health-focused cafés, coworking-friendly spots, and late afternoons around Zen Beach. It suits digital nomads, long-stay visitors, solo travelers, and couples who care more about quiet routines than all-night nightlife. [Out Thailand](https://outthailand.com/guide/where-to-stay-koh-phangan/)

Expect roughly ฿1,500–3,000 per night, or US$45–90, for a typical mid-range stay. [Out Thailand](https://outthailand.com/guide/where-to-stay-koh-phangan/) At the lower end, a 20 September 2026 price snapshot showed Haad Chao Phao Resort around US$25 per night and Sea Garden Resort Haad Chao Phao around US$43. Those are useful reference points, not fixed rates; the same rooms can cost more on weekends and in the dry season. [HotelsCombined](https://www.hotelscombined.com/Place/Sri_Thanu.htm)

Sri Thanu Beach, Zen Beach, yoga studios, vegan and vegetarian restaurants, coffee shops, and casual sunset bars are close enough to reach on foot depending on your exact hotel. This is a better area for unhurried mornings than for quick ferry departures, so plan a taxi if you have luggage.

There is no station or rail service. Thong Sala Pier is the nearest transport hub, about 15–20 minutes away by road in normal conditions, though rain and traffic can stretch that journey.

### Haad Yao, Haad Salad, and Mae Haad

The northwest coast gives you the version of Koh Phangan that many first imagine: pale sand, calm-looking bays, sunset restaurants, and resorts set above the water. Haad Yao, Haad Salad, and Mae Haad suit families, couples, and travelers who want beach access without Haad Rin’s party infrastructure. [Out Thailand](https://outthailand.com/guide/where-to-stay-koh-phangan/)

A typical mid-range room is around ฿2,000–4,000, or US$60–120, though lower prices appear outside high season. [Out Thailand](https://outthailand.com/guide/where-to-stay-koh-phangan/) Maehaad Bay Resort appeared from PHP 3,271 per night in a January 2026 comparison listing, roughly US$55–60 depending on exchange rates. Haad Yao Bayview Resort & Spa appeared from PHP 1,826, roughly US$30–35. Treat both figures as date-specific examples rather than permanent rates. [Trip.com](https://ph.trip.com/hotels/landmark/th/thong-sala-pier.html)

From the right hotel, you can walk to Haad Yao Beach, Haad Salad Beach, Mae Haad Beach, small beachfront restaurants, and the Koh Ma sandbar. Koh Ma is especially useful for travelers who want an easy snorkeling day without committing to a full boat trip.

There is no metro or station. You will arrive by private taxi, songthaew where available, motorbike taxi, rental scooter, or car from Thong Sala. [Phanganist](https://phanganist.com/koh-phangan/thong-sala-pier)

### Haad Rin and Baan Tai

Haad Rin is where the island’s Full Moon Party happens, centered on Haad Rin Nok beach. Baan Tai stretches north along the southeast coast and works as a more spread-out, usually quieter alternative between Haad Rin and Thong Sala. This is the right area if the party is part of your plan, or if you want a cheaper southeast-coast base with easy road access in both directions. [Out Thailand](https://outthailand.com/guide/where-to-stay-koh-phangan/)

On ordinary dates, Haad Rin rooms can start around ฿800–1,500, or US$24–45, though the range becomes far wider near Full Moon Party dates. [Out Thailand](https://outthailand.com/guide/where-to-stay-koh-phangan/) The Sea Resort Haad Rin was listed from ฿991, with a ฿1,100 total shown on one July 2026 booking snapshot. Hotels.com showed Haad Rin rates from roughly US$51 to US$385 or more, a reminder that dates matter more here than almost anywhere else on the island. [Hotels.com](https://www.hotels.com/nh1748548/hotels-in-haad-rin-koh-phangan-thailand/) [The Sea Resort Haad Rin](https://th.hotels.com/en/ho2670198368/the-sea-resort-ko-pha-ngan-thailand/)

You can walk to Haad Rin Nok, Haad Rin Nai, Leela Beach, bars, restaurants, beach shops, and party-night services. On Full Moon week, stay close enough to walk home; late-night taxis are more expensive and less predictable.

Haad Rin Queen’s Pier handles some Koh Samui connections, while road transport links the area with Thong Sala. Schedules vary by operator and season.

## Things to do

### Half-day

**Snorkel at Koh Ma from Mae Haad.** Walk or ride to Mae Haad Beach, then cross the sandbar toward Koh Ma when conditions allow. It is the island’s simplest independent snorkeling option, although conditions vary with weather and water clarity. There is no reliable current price for a Koh Ma-only organized trip in the research, so budget for transport and rental gear rather than quoting a package rate.

**Drive the Than Sadet and east-coast circuit.** Than Sadet waterfall and the quieter east coast make a good half-day if you want roads, jungle, rock pools, and a different feel from the developed west coast. A seven-hour 4x4 Thong Nai Pan–Than Sadet excursion was listed from about ฿1,800, or US$54. Go in the morning, ideally after rain when the waterfall has more water, but avoid stormy conditions. [GetYourGuide](https://www.getyourguide.com/ko-phangan-l5038/national-parks-tc36/)

## Getting around

Koh Phangan has no airport, BTS, metro, or rail network. You arrive by water, then get around by taxi, songthaew, motorbike taxi, rental scooter, or car. [Phanganist](https://phanganist.com/koh-phangan/thong-sala-pier)

The quickest route is flying into Samui Airport, USM, then taking a taxi or minivan to a Samui pier and a ferry or speedboat onward to Koh Phangan. The crossing usually takes around 1–1.5 hours and costs roughly ฿350–550, or US$10–16. [Trip.com](https://www.trip.com/transport/nearby-airports/koh-phangan.html) For a lower-cost route, fly into Surat Thani Airport, URT, and use a combined minivan or coach plus ferry ticket to Thong Sala. Expect around four to five hours in normal conditions, for about ฿620–950, or US$19–28. [Trip.com](https://www.trip.com/transport/nearby-airports/koh-phangan.html)

From Thong Sala Pier, private taxis are commonly around ฿300–500, or US$9–15. Agree the whole fare before departure, especially after dark or for remote beaches. [Phanganist](https://phanganist.com/koh-phangan/thong-sala-pier) Songthaews cost less when they are running, but they become less frequent away from main roads and after sunset. Rental cars cost around ฿1,200–2,500 per day; scooters are convenient but require real confidence on wet, steep, sandy roads. ## Local tips + scams

Start with the manners that make daily interactions easier. Keep your hands to yourself and do not touch anyone’s head. Keep your feet low and avoid pointing them toward people, Buddha images, or sacred objects. When you enter a temple or private home, cover your shoulders and knees. On the island, it is also worth keeping your voice down when speaking with staff, drivers, or shop owners; calm conversation gets you further than public frustration.

For transport, agree the price first. A private taxi from Thong Sala is often quoted around ฿300–500, but prices can rise late at night, in rain, and during Full Moon Party week. Ask, “How much total to Haad Yao?” or “How much total to Haad Rin?” before luggage goes in the vehicle. [Phanganist](https://phanganist.com/koh-phangan/thong-sala-pier)

Scooter disputes are another predictable problem. Before leaving a rental shop, record a slow video of every panel, wheel, mirror, light, and existing scratch. Wear a helmet, never ride after drinking, and do not assume the road from Thong Sala to a remote beach will be forgiving after rain. This is less about proving someone dishonest and more about avoiding a stressful argument at the end of your trip.

At Full Moon Party, keep your passport and main bank card in your room, watch your drink, and settle your ride home before the beach gets crowded. Party crowds can reach tens of thousands, and the practical risks are theft, drink safety, and impaired driving rather than the party itself. [Thai Holiday Guide](https://www.thaiholidayguide.com/events/full-moon-party-koh-phangan/)

A local-style sunset move: stay around Sri Thanu, then head toward Zen Beach before dusk. It is not secret, and it should not be treated like one. But it is an easy place to slow down, buy a drink nearby, and watch the west coast change color without riding all the way to Haad Salad. [Out Thailand](https://outthailand.com/guide/where-to-stay-koh-phangan/)

## FAQ

### Is Koh Phangan safe for solo female travelers?

Yes, with normal island caution. Stay in established areas, use a known taxi or pre-arranged transport after dark, and avoid riding a scooter after alcohol. Full Moon Party nights need more planning: carry minimal valuables, watch your drink, and know your route home before you go out.

### How many days do I need in Koh Phangan?

Plan four to five nights for a first trip. That gives you a west-coast beach day, a boat day to Ang Thong or Sail Rock, time around Sri Thanu or Thong Sala, and at least one weather buffer. Add nights if you are diving or travelling during October and November.

### Is Koh Phangan expensive?

It can be, especially in Haad Rin around Full Moon Party, and at Christmas and New Year. Backpackers can manage on ฿1,200–2,200 per day, or US$36–66, while a comfortable mid-range stay is closer to ฿4,000–6,000, or US$120–180 daily. [Nomadic Matt](https://www.nomadicmatt.com/travel-guides/thailand-travel-tips/ko-phangan/)

### What is the best area for first-time visitors?

Thong Sala is the easiest base if you want ferry access, street food, ATMs, shops, and straightforward transport around the island. Sri Thanu is better if you prefer cafés, sunset beaches, yoga, and a slower west-coast routine, but you will rely more on taxis, scooters, or a rental car. [Out Thailand](https://outthailand.com/guide/where-to-stay-koh-phangan/)

### Do I need cash in Koh Phangan?

Yes. Resorts, larger restaurants, and dive shops may accept cards, but taxis, food stalls, market vendors, smaller beach restaurants, and independent rentals often prefer Thai baht. Bring enough cash for daily transport and meals, especially if you are staying outside Thong Sala or travelling during rain.

### Do I need to plan around the Full Moon Party?

Only if you are staying in Haad Rin or Baan Tai, taking ferries at peak times, or hoping for a quiet night. Party dates can push up room prices, reduce availability, and make late-night transport harder. If you want calm beaches, book the northwest coast instead; if you want the party, stay walking distance from Haad Rin Nok.
