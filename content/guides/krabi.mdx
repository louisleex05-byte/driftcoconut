## Quick facts

- **Airport:** Krabi International Airport (**KBV**) is the arrival point for Ao Nang, Krabi Town, Railay and Klong Muang. [Travel Guides Tip](https://travelguidestip.com/krabi-airport-transfer/)
- **Currency:** Thai baht (฿ / THB). On 4 September 2026, the planning benchmark was around **฿32.77 to US$1**, making ฿1,000 roughly **US$30.52**. Exchange rates move daily. [All Rates Today](https://allratestoday.com/central-bank-rates-api/bot/)
- **Time:** Krabi runs on Indochina Time, UTC+7, with no daylight-saving change.
- **Language:** Thai is the local language. English is common at hotels, tour counters, beach restaurants and transport desks, but less reliable once you leave the tourist coast.
- **Visa note:** From **15 September 2026**, US, UK, Australian, Japanese, South Korean and many EU passport holders are reported to receive a 30-day visa-exempt stay, rather than visa on arrival. Check official Thai immigration guidance before flying; entry rules can shift. [Legit.ng](https://www.legit.ng/world/asia/1728367-thailand-cuts-visa-free-stay-60-30-days-60-countries-announces-commencement-date/)
- **Mid-range spending:** Expect around **฿3,000–5,500 / US$92–168 per person daily** for a shared mid-range room, three meals and local transport, before full-day island trips. [Visit Thailand Today](https://www.visitthailandtoday.com/guides/where-to-stay-in-krabi)
- **Getting to Railay:** There is no road connection. Take a longtail from Ao Nang; the usual shared fare is around **฿100 / US$3.05 each way**. [Krabi Pointer](https://krabipointer.com/guides/krabi-ao-nang-vs-railay/)

## When to go

Krabi’s calendar is simple: dry-season reliability comes from November through April; lower prices and a looser pace arrive with the May–October green season. The trade-off is the sea. In the wetter months, rain is not merely an afternoon inconvenience—boat itineraries can change, waves can build, and a day at Hong Islands may need to become a slow lunch in Ao Nang instead. [Thong Yib Thong Yod](https://thongyibthongyod.com/blog/best-time-to-visit-krabi/)

**January** is dry, around 33°C with roughly 30 mm of rain, and very busy after New Year. **February** stays dry and sunny at around 34°C with about 45 mm of rain; it is one of the most dependable months for island days. Hotel rates and popular boats sit at their annual high in both months. [Thong Yib Thong Yod](https://thongyibthongyod.com/blog/best-time-to-visit-krabi/)

In **March**, temperatures remain around 34°C and rain rises to roughly 100 mm. It is still good for the water, though the midday heat starts to feel heavy on temple steps and exposed beaches. **April** is the hottest period, around 34°C with approximately 145 mm of rain. Songkran runs **13–15 April 2026** and again **13–15 April 2027**; expect water throwing around the resort areas, crowded roads, and short holiday price spikes. [Thai Embassy Permanent Mission to the UN](https://unmissionnewyork.thaiembassy.org/en/content/official-holidays-2026)

**May** marks the monsoon transition: around 33°C, roughly 170 mm of rain, more unsettled afternoons and lower room rates. **June** and **July** remain warm at about 32°C, with roughly 195–200 mm of rain. These are useful value months if you can leave a day or two open for weather changes; mornings are often the better window for boats. [Thong Yib Thong Yod](https://thongyibthongyod.com/blog/best-time-to-visit-krabi/)

By **August**, rainfall averages around 265 mm and the sea can be less predictable. Maya Bay is usually listed as closed from **1 August to 30 September** for ecological recovery, so do not build a September Phi Phi itinerary around it without checking the current national-park notice. [Trip Thai Tour](https://www.tripthaitour.com/blog/things-to-do-in-krabi) **September** averages roughly 275 mm of rain, while **October**, at about 325 mm, is the wettest planning month. Both are low season and often the cheapest time to book a room, but boat tours may be delayed or cancelled. In early September 2026, the Thai Meteorological Department warned of heavy rain, flash-flood and landslide risk, with 1–2 metre waves and higher waves in thunderstorms along the Andaman coast. [Thailand Meteorological Department](https://www5.tmd.go.th/en/forecast/daily/040920260600)

<GuidePhoto slot="phiPhi" />

**November** improves quickly: roughly 31°C, around 180 mm of rain, and a noticeable return of reliable sea days. It is shoulder season at first, then rates rise towards the end of the month. Loy Krathong falls on **14 November 2027**; the 2026 date is uncertain in available sources, variously listed as 24 or 25 November. [Destination Thailand](https://destinationthailand.io/thailand-festivals-and-public-holidays/) **December** brings lower rainfall, around 70 mm, calm-water conditions and peak-season pricing from mid-month into early January. [Thong Yib Thong Yod](https://thongyibthongyod.com/blog/best-time-to-visit-krabi/)

Krabi is not Northern Thailand’s main burning-season destination, but check live AQI during your trip. Coastal weather, regional haze and local conditions can still affect visibility.

## Where to stay

### Ao Nang

Ao Nang is Krabi’s practical base: a beachfront road full of longtail queues, casual restaurants, pharmacies, dive shops, cafés and tour counters, with enough accommodation choice to make last-minute changes manageable. It works well for first-time visitors, families, short-stay digital nomads and anyone who prefers to step outside and have dinner, cash machines and island boats within a few minutes’ walk. [Visit Thailand Today](https://www.visitthailandtoday.com/guides/where-to-stay-in-krabi)

A typical mid-range room costs around **฿1,500–3,000 / US$46–92 per night**, though December through February can push the same property well beyond that range. [Visit Thailand Today](https://www.visitthailandtoday.com/guides/where-to-stay-in-krabi) **Aonang Cliff Beach Resort** typically appears around **US$75–130**, while **Krabi La Playa Resort** is commonly around **US$80–140**, depending on season and room category. [Travel Guides Tip](https://travelguidestip.com/krabi-hotels/)

From central Ao Nang you can walk to Ao Nang Beach, the Railay longtail departure area, restaurants along the beach road, exchange booths, tour agencies and evening bars. The nearest useful transport point is the Ao Nang beach longtail area, plus the airport shuttle and Ao Nang–Krabi Town songthaew route. [Krabi Pointer](https://krabipointer.com/guides/getting-around-krabi/)

Ao Nang is not the place for empty beaches, but it is the most efficient place to sleep when you have limited days and want your itinerary to run without much friction.

### Railay

Railay is a car-free peninsula framed by limestone walls, reached only by longtail boat. It suits climbers, couples, photographers and travellers who want to wake up near the sand rather than beside a traffic-heavy road. You trade convenience for atmosphere: arrivals depend on boats, luggage may get wet at the landing, and your movement follows paths rather than roads. [Travel Guides Tip](https://travelguidestip.com/best-hotels-in-krabi/)

Mid-range accommodation generally falls around **฿2,500–5,000 / US$76–153 per night**. [Visit Thailand Today](https://www.visitthailandtoday.com/guides/where-to-stay-in-krabi) **Sand Sea Resort Railay** is often around **US$90–180**. **Rayavadee** sits firmly in luxury territory rather than mid-range, with observed 2026 prices around **US$380–480** in one guide and higher during peak dates. [Travel Guides Tip](https://travelguidestip.com/krabi-hotels/)

Walk from Railay West to Railay East for restaurants and bars, then continue towards Phra Nang Cave Beach, viewpoints, lagoons and climbing walls. The practical transport point is Railay West or Railay East’s longtail landing area. Shared boats from Ao Nang are typically about **฿100 / US$3.05 each way**, weather permitting. [Krabi Pointer](https://krabipointer.com/guides/krabi-ao-nang-vs-railay/)

Stay here if you want the cliffs outside your room. Stay in Ao Nang if you want a more flexible restaurant choice, easier airport logistics and a simpler departure day.

### Krabi Town

Krabi Town is the provincial centre rather than a beach resort. It is where you come for riverside evenings, local food, night-market walks and a lower nightly rate; it makes particular sense for backpackers, food-first travellers and anyone arriving late at KBV or leaving early the next morning. [Visit Thailand Today](https://www.visitthailandtoday.com/guides/where-to-stay-in-krabi)

Expect a typical mid-range room range of **฿500–1,200 / US$15–37 per night**. [Visit Thailand Today](https://www.visitthailandtoday.com/guides/where-to-stay-in-krabi) **Nap Krabi Hotel** has appeared at around ₹3,297 per night in current regional listings. Its exact USD price changes with exchange rates and booking dates. [MakeMyTrip](https://www.makemytrip.global/hotels-international/en-ar/thailand/resorts-in-krabi-p/) A second Krabi Town hotel price was not reliably confirmed in the research, so check live rates rather than treating older search listings as a promise.

You can walk to the Walking Street and night-market areas, local restaurants, temples, riverside sections and everyday Thai shops. Krabi Town’s useful transport nodes are the bus terminal, Talad Kao area, airport bus stops and ferry-pier connections. KBV is roughly 15–30 minutes away depending on traffic and pickup point. [Travel Guides Tip](https://travelguidestip.com/krabi-airport-transfer/)

Krabi Town is a good reset between island days, but it is not the best single base if beach access is your priority.

### Klong Muang and Tubkaek

Klong Muang and Tubkaek sit north of Ao Nang, with quieter beaches, larger resorts and a slower evening rhythm. This is the right coast for families wanting space, couples planning a resort stay, and travellers who would rather hear water than bar music after dinner. [Visit Thailand Today](https://www.visitthailandtoday.com/guides/where-to-stay-in-krabi)

## Local tips + scams

Keep your feet low. In temples and around Buddha images, avoid pointing the soles of your feet at people or sacred objects. Keep your tone low too, especially in temples, ruins and public places. If you need to settle a fare disagreement, a calm voice works better than an argument.

Dress properly before entering a temple: shoulders and knees should be fully covered, and shoes come off before entering temple buildings. Krabi is relaxed on the beach, but temple clothing is not beach clothing. Keep food choices sensible as well—avoid wildlife and novelty “weird food” encounters that are not part of normal local eating.

The first recurring problem is an **unclear taxi or longtail fare**. Ask whether the price is per person or per boat, confirm the destination, and settle the total before departing. Krabi taxis often do not run meters, and a vague quote at the beach can become an expensive ride later. [Arrival Brief](https://arrivalbrief.com/cities/krabi/)

The second is a **scooter or jet-ski damage claim**. Film the vehicle or hull from every angle before taking it. Photograph scratches, wheels, mirrors and the fuel gauge, keep the paperwork, and walk away from any rental that presses you to leave your passport or skips the inspection. Road accidents are a much larger risk than most rental disputes. [Arrival Brief](https://arrivalbrief.com/cities/krabi/)

The third is a **tour price that leaves out key costs**. A cheap boat ticket may exclude national-park fees, hotel pickup, lunch, snorkeling gear or insurance. Compare inclusions, not just the first price shown, and keep the booking confirmation on your phone. [Machu Picchu Travel Guide](https://www.machupicchu.org/krabi-travel-guide-2026-beaches-islands-limestone-cliffs.htm)

One useful local habit: sleep in Ao Nang and visit Railay early or late. The shared longtail is around ฿100 each way, but boats depend on weather and passenger loading. You gain Railay’s cliffs without making every airport run, dinner plan and rain-day decision depend on a boat. [Krabi Pointer](https://krabipointer.com/guides/krabi-ao-nang-vs-railay/)

For food, skip the tourist-strip Western menus and eat what Krabi is actually known for. Southern Thai **khanom jeen** — thin fermented rice noodles served with a choice of coconut-milk curry, spicy fish curry (**nam ya**), or peanut-based sauces (**nam prik**) — is the region's honest breakfast and lunch. Locals eat it with a plate of free vegetables (long beans, cucumber, pickled mustard greens) piled on top. The best clusters are the **Krabi Town morning market** near Maharaj Road and small stalls around Ao Nang's Soi 2. Expect ฿40–60 (US$1.20–1.80) per bowl, cash only. Ask for it "**mai phet**" if you can't handle Southern-heat spice.

## FAQ

### Is Krabi safe for solo female travellers?

Krabi is generally low-risk for violent crime against visitors, but use normal coastal-travel judgment. Take reputable transport after dark, avoid isolated beach paths alone late at night, keep valuables off the sand, and do not ride a scooter without proper licensing and insurance. Main practical risks are road accidents, unclear fares, weather and water conditions. [Arrival Brief](https://arrivalbrief.com/cities/krabi/)

### How many days do I need in Krabi?

Stay at least three nights for Railay, one island day and one slower inland or beach day. Five to six nights is better: it gives you room for Hong Islands, Four Islands, Tiger Cave Temple or Emerald Pool, plus a weather buffer. In green season, that spare day is especially useful.

### Is Krabi expensive?

Krabi can be inexpensive until you start stacking speedboats, private transfers and peak-season beachfront rooms. Backpackers can plan around US$35–65 daily, while a comfortable mid-range trip is usually US$90–170 per person. Luxury beach resorts and private boat days can take the figure beyond US$250. [Travel Guides Tip](https://travelguidestip.com/krabi-travel-budget-2026-how-much-does-it-cost/)

### What is the best area for first-time visitors?

Stay in central Ao Nang. You can walk to restaurants, tour desks, pharmacies, exchange booths and the Railay longtail point. It also has the broadest range of rooms and the simplest airport connection, which matters more than a perfectly quiet beach on a first Krabi trip. [Visit Thailand Today](https://www.visitthailandtoday.com/guides/where-to-stay-in-krabi)

### Do I need cash in Krabi?

Yes. Use cards for established hotels, larger restaurants and some tour operators, but carry baht for longtails, songthaews, park fees, markets, casual eateries and tips. A useful first-day cash amount is enough for the airport transfer, food and a boat fare, then top up from an ATM in Ao Nang or Krabi Town.

### Can I visit Maya Bay from Krabi in September?

Usually not. Current 2026 guidance lists Maya Bay’s annual ecological closure from **1 August to 30 September**. You can still visit Phi Phi, but do not book a September trip that promises a Maya Bay landing until the national park or operator confirms it is open. [Trip Thai Tour](https://www.tripthaitour.com/blog/things-to-do-in-krabi)
