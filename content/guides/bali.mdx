## Quick facts

- **Airport:** I Gusti Ngurah Rai International Airport (**DPS**), also called Denpasar Airport or Bali Airport. [Bali Malayali](https://www.balimalayali.com/destinations/guide/bali-airport-guide)
- **Currency:** Indonesian rupiah (**IDR**, written Rp). On 1 September 2026, US$1 was about **IDR 17,740**, so IDR 100,000 was roughly US$5.64 before exchange or card fees. [Databoks](https://databoks.katadata.co.id/en/market/statistics/32ec67c22c32882/rupiah-exchange-rate-strengthens-to-idr-17704-per-us-dollar-tuesday-25-august-2026)
- **Time:** WITA, Central Indonesia Time (UTC+8), with no daylight-saving time. [UNCERTAIN"”confirm against a current time-zone authority before publishing.]
- **Languages:** Bahasa Indonesia is the national language; Balinese is widely used in local life, while English is common in the main visitor areas. [UNCERTAIN"”confirm against a current Indonesian government or tourism source before publishing.]
- **Tourist levy:** Foreign visitors pay a one-off Bali levy of **IDR 150,000** (about US$8.46) per trip, separate from visa fees. Pay only via the official Love Bali government platform. [Wego](https://blog.wego.com/bali-tourist-levy/)
- **Visa snapshot:** US, UK, EU, Australian, Japanese and South Korean passport holders are listed as eligible for visa on arrival or e-VOA for ordinary tourism. The B1 visa costs **IDR 500,000** (about US$28.19), starts at 30 days and may be extended once to 60 days. Confirm nationality-specific rules directly with Indonesia Immigration before flying. [VisaField](https://visafield.com/visa/indonesia/e-voa/visa_guide/)
- **A realistic mid-range day:** Plan around **IDR 1.2"“2.5 million** (US$77"“160) once you include a decent room, three meals, local rides and the occasional paid sight. [Go2 Bali](https://go2-bali.com/blog/bali-cost-breakdown/)
- **Getting around:** There is no metro, BTS or airport rail link. Bali works by road: ride-hailing, metered taxis, scooters and hired drivers. [Nomads in Asia](https://nomadsinasia.com/blog/bali-transport-guide/)

## When to go

Bali stays warm all year, usually around 28"“31°C on the coast. The useful distinction is not temperature but rain, humidity, traffic and room rates. The drier stretch runs broadly from **May to October**; from **November to April**, showers are more frequent and often arrive in the afternoon or evening rather than sitting over the island all day. [Bali Travel Guide Plus](https://balitravelguideplus.com/practical/weather-by-month)

January is wet"”roughly 24"“31°C and often 15"“20 rainy days"”but quieter after the New Year rush, so rooms can be better value after 5 January. February remains hot, humid and wet, with 14"“20 rainy days in the cited seasonal guide; it suits a flexible villa, spa or café-focused stay better than a beach-only itinerary. [When to Wander](https://www.when-to-wander.com/destinations/bali/wet-season/) [Bali Travel Guide Plus](https://balitravelguideplus.com/practical/weather-by-month)

March is still rainy, though conditions tend to improve later in the month. Build your itinerary around **Nyepi on Thursday 19 March 2026**: roads and Ngurah Rai airport close during the 24-hour Day of Silence, while ogoh-ogoh processions take place on the evening of 18 March. In 2027, Nyepi falls on **Monday 8 March**. [Tempo](https://en.tempo.co/read/2080212/bali-slates-56-festivals-cultural-agendas-in-2026-heres-the-complete-list) [When to Wander](https://www.when-to-wander.com/destinations/bali/wet-season/)

April is a useful transition month: around 31°C, roughly eight rainy days on average, and generally better value than high summer, although Easter and school holidays lift demand. May is one of the easiest all-round choices"”around 30°C, about five rainy days, greener landscapes than late dry season, and lower rates before the June"“August rush. [Bali Travel Guide Plus](https://balitravelguideplus.com/practical/weather-by-month)

June is dry and warm at around 29°C, but hotel rates begin climbing. **Galungan falls on Wednesday 17 June 2026** and **Kuningan on Saturday 27 June 2026**; the Bali Arts Festival runs from **13 June to 11 July 2026**. July and August bring the driest, least humid conditions"”about 28°C and around two rainy days in the cited guide"”but also the island's heaviest traffic and highest mainstream rates. Book hotels, drivers, airport transfers and Nusa Penida boats early. [Travjoy](https://travjoy.com/blog/bali-festivals-a-complete-calendar-of-events-and-ceremonies) [Bali Travel Guide Plus](https://balitravelguideplus.com/practical/weather-by-month)

<GuidePhoto slot="nusaPenida" />

September is the sweet spot I would choose from Thailand: around 29°C, still dry with about three rainy days, and usually less crowded after the Australian and European summer break. Premium surf villas do not suddenly become cheap, but the pressure eases. The 2026 calendar includes the Bali International Airshow, Lembongan Surf and Music Festival and Taman Ayun Barong Festival. October stays warm at roughly 30°C, with rain beginning to return; it can be good value before Christmas and includes the Ubud Writers & Readers Festival, Nusa Dua Festival and Klungkung Heritage Festival. [Bali Travel Guide Plus](https://balitravelguideplus.com/practical/weather-by-month) [Love Bali](https://lovebali.baliprov.go.id/article/detail/1768353645163/bali-calendar-of-events-2026:-celebrating-culture,-creativity,-and-quality-tourism-throughout-the-year)

November has around 10 rainy days and lower room rates; December has about 13, then prices rise sharply from 24 December through 5 January. For 2027 cultural planning, Galungan/Kuningan fall on **13/23 January** and **11/21 August**. [Bali Travel Guide Plus](https://balitravelguideplus.com/practical/weather-by-month) [Bali Shortlist](https://balishortlist.com/events/)

Bali does not usually have a predictable local burning season like Sumatra or Kalimantan. Rain, surf, traffic and holiday demand are more reliable planning factors. Haze and volcanic ash can still disrupt visibility or flights, so check airline notices and official volcanic updates close to travel. [The Traveler](https://www.thetraveler.org/u-s-urges-increased-caution-for-travelers-to-indonesia/)

## Where to stay

### Ubud

Ubud is Bali's inland cultural base: temple courtyards, rice landscapes, galleries, yoga rooms and cafés sit within a short but often traffic-slow drive of one another. It suits first-timers who care more about art, food and temple days than a beach outside the hotel door; it also works well for couples, wellness travellers, families not seeking surf, and digital nomads who prefer a slower rhythm. [Machu Picchu](https://www.machupicchu.org/ubud-travel-guide-2026-balis-cultural-heart.htm)

For a mid-range boutique hotel or small villa, budget **IDR 700,000"“1.5 million** (US$45"“96) a night. **Alaya Resort Ubud** and **Komaneka at Bisma** are solid named reference points at the more polished end of the category, though rates move sharply by date and should be checked before booking. [Go2 Bali](https://go2-bali.com/blog/bali-cost-breakdown/)

Stay near Jalan Monkey Forest or Jalan Bisma if walking matters. Ubud Palace, Ubud Art Market, cafés, spas, museums and the Monkey Forest area are then close enough to reach without building every outing around a car. There is no rail or metro; use private drivers or Grab/Gojek where collection is permitted, with wider bus links on the Trans Metro Dewata network. [Machu Picchu](https://www.machupicchu.org/ubud-travel-guide-2026-balis-cultural-heart.htm) [Tribun Travel](https://travel.tribunnews.com/ticketing/144407/keliling-bali-rp-4400-rute-dan-jadwal-lengkap-trans-metro-dewata-2026)

### Seminyak

Seminyak is where a beach morning can turn into lunch on Jalan Kayu Aya, a browse through boutiques, and sunset by the water without changing districts. It is a sensible first base for couples, food-focused travellers and visitors who want south-Bali comfort without Uluwatu's longer, more isolated road journeys.

Plan on **IDR 700,000"“1.5 million** (US$45"“96) for a mid-range room, with beachfront addresses costing more. **Amadea Resort & Villas Seminyak** and **U Paasha Seminyak Bali** are useful hotel benchmarks; search your exact dates because live prices vary considerably by season. [Go2 Bali](https://go2-bali.com/blog/bali-cost-breakdown/)

The best walking pockets sit around Eat Street/Jalan Kayu Aya and toward Petitenget: Seminyak Beach, restaurants, cafés, boutiques and beach clubs are nearby. Bali has no local rail station here. From DPS, a taxi or ride-hail transfer usually takes **30"“45 minutes** in ordinary conditions, though traffic decides the final answer. [Nomads in Asia](https://nomadsinasia.com/blog/bali-transport-guide/)

### Canggu

Canggu is Bali's surf-and-café district, with Batu Bolong, Echo Beach and Berawa pulling together co-working spaces, gyms, villas, beach bars and a very real traffic problem. It suits surfers, remote workers, groups and travellers who want a social base; skip it if you want quiet lanes or easy island-wide sightseeing.

A workable mid-range range is **IDR 650,000"“1.3 million** (US$40"“80) a night, although popular beach-adjacent rooms can rise far beyond that in peak season. **Eastin Ashta Resort Canggu** and **The Komu Canggu Bali** are named options to compare once you have dates. [Travel Guides Tip](https://travelguidestip.com/bali-travel-budget/)

Batu Bolong Beach, Echo Beach, Berawa's cafés, gyms, restaurants and beach venues are locally walkable only if your hotel is genuinely close; tropical heat and narrow traffic-heavy roads change the experience quickly. There is no rail or metro. Scooters and ride-hailing dominate, and the airport transfer commonly takes **45"“75 minutes**. [Nomads in Asia](https://nomadsinasia.com/blog/bali-transport-guide/)

### Sanur

Sanur keeps a quieter east-coast pace, centred on its beachfront promenade, sunrise walks and gentler water in many sections. It is the practical choice for families, older travellers, couples who want a calmer beach base, divers and anyone catching boats onward to Nusa Lembongan or Nusa Penida.

Expect **IDR 700,000"“1.5 million** (US$45"“96) for a mid-range 3"“4-star room; beachfront resorts can go higher. **ARTOTEL Sanur "“ Bali** and **Prime Plaza Hotel Sanur** are useful mid-range references, with exact rates dependent on travel dates. [Go2 Bali](https://go2-bali.com/blog/bali-cost-breakdown/)

The beach path, cafés, restaurants, Sindhu Market area and boat access points are the reason to stay here. There is no rail or metro station, but DPS is typically a manageable **25"“40-minute** car ride. [Nomads in Asia](https://nomadsinasia.com/blog/bali-transport-guide/)

## Getting around

Bali rewards realistic timing. A map can make a 10-kilometre trip look harmless; south-Bali traffic can make it the main event of your afternoon. From DPS, expect roughly **30"“45 minutes to Seminyak**, **45"“75 minutes to Canggu**, **25"“40 minutes to Sanur**, and **75"“120 minutes to Ubud**. App cars run around IDR 80,000"“120,000 to Seminyak, IDR 120,000"“180,000 to Canggu, IDR 80,000"“120,000 to Sanur and IDR 200,000"“280,000 to Ubud; taxi quotes can be higher. [Nomads in Asia](https://nomadsinasia.com/blog/bali-transport-guide/)

At arrivals, use the official taxi counter, a verified hotel transfer or the designated Grab/Gojek pickup area. If you arrive late, have children, or simply do not want to negotiate after a flight, book <AffiliateLink type="welcomePickups">Welcome Pickups</AffiliateLink> ahead.

## Local tips + scams

At temples, dress for the place you are entering, not for the weather outside. Sarongs and sashes are often required; cover shoulders and knees when requested, and follow any entry rules posted at the gate. [UNCERTAIN"”confirm exact site rules before publishing.]

Watch your feet. Canang sari offerings"”small palm-leaf trays with flowers and incense"”sit on pavements, steps and shop entrances. Step around them rather than through them. When handing over money or receiving an item, use your right hand when practical, and do not touch somebody's head. These details make the difference between arriving as a visitor and moving through Bali with basic respect. [UNCERTAIN"”confirm with a current Balinese cultural authority before publishing.]

Three problems are worth knowing before they become your story. First, avoid money changers offering rates that look dramatically better than a bank or reputable counter: note-palming and recounting tricks remain reported around Kuta, Legian and Seminyak. Count your own notes and walk away from an unreal rate. [SB Excursion](https://www.sbexcursion.com/bali/en/journal/bali-safety-scams-and-health)

Second, scooter renters can claim damage that was already on the bike. Film the whole scooter"”panels, tyres, mirrors, fuel level and every scratch"”before you leave, then keep the clip until your deposit is returned. Third, do not hand cash to someone demanding an on-the-spot "police fine." Ask for formal identification and written procedure instead. [SB Excursion](https://www.sbexcursion.com/bali/en/journal/bali-safety-scams-and-health)

Here is the small planning habit I would borrow from living and travelling around Southeast Asia: choose your hotel according to tomorrow morning, not today's map pin. A Ubud room plus a Sanur or Seminyak room often gives you more beach, more temple time and less time staring at brake lights than staying in one villa and crossing the island daily. The airport-to-Ubud and airport-to-Canggu transfer ranges make the point clearly. [Nomads in Asia](https://nomadsinasia.com/blog/bali-transport-guide/)

Keep mosquito repellent in your day bag. Dengue is a concern, rabies remains a reason not to touch dogs, cats or monkeys, and a bite or scratch should be washed with soap and running water for 15 minutes before seeking same-day medical advice. [SB Excursion](https://www.sbexcursion.com/bali/en/journal/bali-safety-scams-and-health)

## FAQ

### Is Bali safe for solo female travellers?

Generally, yes, with ordinary travel awareness. Use verified ride-hailing or taxis, avoid isolated lanes after dark, keep an eye on drinks in nightlife settings, and take scooter safety seriously. Common practical issues are petty theft, bag snatching, scams, drink safety and traffic accidents rather than targeted violent crime. Sanur and central Ubud can feel simpler than remote cliff roads or deep Canggu lanes. [Qantas](https://www.qantas.com/travelinsider/en/explore/asia/indonesia/bali/travel-tips-bali)

### How many days do I need in Bali?

Give Bali **seven to ten nights** if it is your first trip. That leaves room for an inland base, a beach base, a rest day and at least one longer excursion without turning every morning into a transfer. With four or five nights, choose south Bali or Ubud and nearby sights rather than trying to cover the entire island. [Nomads in Asia](https://nomadsinasia.com/blog/bali-transport-guide/)

### Is Bali expensive?

It can be inexpensive or very expensive, depending on your address and habits. Backpackers can plan around **US$25"“50** a day; a comfortable mid-range trip is usually **US$80"“150**; luxury resorts, private villas, drivers, beach clubs and premium dining can push the day above **US$300**. July, August and Christmas/New Year are the periods most likely to stretch the room budget. [Travel Guides Tip](https://travelguidestip.com/is-bali-expensive/) [Chill Bali Trips](https://www.chillbalitrips.com/blog/bali-on-a-budget/) [TripPick](https://trippick.co/en/destinations/bali-id/cost/)

### What is the best area for first-time visitors?

Choose **Seminyak** if you want one easy beach base with restaurants, shopping, a broad hotel range and a relatively manageable transfer from DPS. Choose **Ubud** if temple visits, art, rice fields and a slower setting matter more than staying beside the sea. A split stay"”Ubud first, then Seminyak or Sanur"”usually gives a fuller first trip. [Machu Picchu](https://www.machupicchu.org/ubud-travel-guide-2026-balis-cultural-heart.htm) [Nomads in Asia](https://nomadsinasia.com/blog/bali-transport-guide/)

### Do I need cash in Bali?

Yes, but not only cash. Cards work well at established hotels, restaurants, beach clubs and larger shops. Carry rupiah for temple tickets, small warungs, parking, markets, tips, rural attractions and some transport. Many attraction gates still lean heavily on cash and may charge separately for parking or sarongs, so ATM access remains useful. [SB Excursion](https://www.sbexcursion.com/bali/en/journal/bali-entrance-fees-2026)

<GuidePhoto slot="warung" />

### Can I drink tap water in Bali?

Do not treat untreated tap water as drinking water. Use sealed bottled water or reputable filtered/refill water, and use normal food-hygiene judgment. Bring a refill bottle if your accommodation provides filtered water; it is a small way to cut plastic without taking a health gamble. [Wego](https://blog.wego.com/is-it-safe-to-travel-to-bali-2026/)

### What should I know about Nyepi?

Nyepi is not a festival you can simply watch from outside: it is Bali's Day of Silence. On **19 March 2026** and **8 March 2027**, roads and DPS airport close during the observance, and hotel guests are generally expected to remain on property. Arrive before it starts or schedule around it; the ogoh-ogoh parades on the preceding evening are the public-facing moment.
