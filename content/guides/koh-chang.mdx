## Quick facts

- **Gateway:** Koh Chang has no commercial airport. You fly into Trat Airport (**TDX**) on the mainland, then continue by minivan or taxi and ferry to the island. [I Am Koh Chang](https://iamkohchang.com/getting-here/trat-airport.html)
- **Currency:** Thai baht (**฿ / THB**). Use around **฿33.3 to US$1** for September 2026 planning, then check the live rate before exchanging. [Bank of Thailand](https://app.bot.or.th/BTWS_STAT/statistics/BOTWEBSTAT.aspx?reportID=123&language=eng)
- **Time:** Thailand uses ICT, UTC+7, and does not observe daylight saving time.
- **Language:** Thai is the everyday language. English is widely understood in resorts, dive shops, ferry offices, and the west-coast beach areas.
- **Visa note:** From 15 September 2026, US, UK, Australian, Japanese, and listed EU passport holders are eligible for visa-exempt tourism entry for up to 30 days, subject to immigration conditions. It is not a visa-on-arrival arrangement. [Tourism Authority of Thailand](https://www.tatnews.org/2026/09/thailand-introduces-new-30-day-and-15-day-visa-exemption-rules-from-15-september/)
- **Daily mid-range spend:** Budget around **฿3,000–5,300 (US$90–159)** for a room, three meals, and local transport before snorkeling tours, diving, alcohol, and private transfers. [Explore Koh Chang](https://explorekohchang.com/koh-chang/where-to-stay/) [I Am Koh Chang](https://iamkohchang.com/getting-here/koh-chang-taxi-fares-songtaew.html)
- **Transport reality:** There is no BTS, metro, rail line, or dependable app-taxi coverage. You will use songthaews, hotel drivers, arranged taxis, or a scooter. [I Am Koh Chang](https://iamkohchang.com/getting-here/koh-chang-taxi-fares-songtaew.html)

## When to go

Koh Chang runs on a clear seasonal split. December through February is the period to choose if you want dry beach days, calmer ferry crossings, and the best odds for Koh Rang snorkeling. January averages 31.9°C in the daytime and 22.2°C at night, with about 51 mm of rain. February stays similarly dry at around 32.0°C/22.9°C and 50–59 mm of rain. Those are peak months, so beachfront rooms, ferries, snorkeling boats, and airport transfers cost more and book earlier. [Siam Good](https://siamgood.com/koh-chang/best-time-to-visit/) [Staysion](https://staysion.com/journal/best-time-to-visit-koh-chang)

March remains workable, with highs around 32.6°C and rain in the 83–140 mm range, but the heat builds. April reaches about 33.3°C by day, with more afternoon showers. Prices often ease after the holiday period, although Songkran creates a short demand spike. Songkran falls on **13–15 April 2026** and **13–15 April 2027**. If you travel then, expect water celebrations, busier transport, and less hotel availability around the island’s main beach areas. [Siam Good](https://siamgood.com/koh-chang/best-time-to-visit/) [ThailandHop](https://www.thailandhop.com/events)

May begins the wet turn. Rain can reach 351–420 mm, seas become less predictable, and inter-island trips start to thin out. June through September is the true monsoon block: June can receive 598–777 mm, July reaches as much as 1,024 mm in some climate data, August averages around 687 mm, and September remains very wet at roughly 500–523 mm. Rooms are cheapest then, and the island is quieter, but you need flexible days and low expectations for boat trips. [Siam Good](https://siamgood.com/koh-chang/best-time-to-visit/) [Weather2Travel](https://www.weather2travel.com/thailand/ko-chang/climate/) [Koh Chang Taxis](https://www.kohchangtaxis.com/guide/koh-chang-weather-seasons)

October is a transition month, with rainfall falling toward 300–310 mm and some services restarting from 1 October. November is the value month to watch: calmer sea, rainfall around 55–109 mm, and high-season rates beginning to return. Loy Krathong falls on **24 November 2026**. The expected 2027 date is **14 November**, though local observance can vary with the lunar calendar. [Koh Chang Taxis](https://www.kohchangtaxis.com/guide/koh-chang-weather-seasons) [Fan Club Thailand](https://fanclubthailand.co.uk/thailand-festivals/)

Northern Thailand’s February–April haze is not Koh Chang’s main air-quality problem. This is Trat Province, on the eastern Gulf; the practical concerns are rain, rough water, steep roads, and mosquitoes in the wet months. [Koh Chang Taxis](https://www.kohchangtaxis.com/guide/koh-chang-weather-seasons)

## Where to stay

### White Sand Beach

White Sand Beach is Koh Chang’s easiest first base: the island’s most developed beach strip, with a wider spread of resorts, restaurants, massage shops, minimarts, tour desks, and evening places to eat. Stay here if you want your first few days to be straightforward and do not want to rely on a scooter for every meal or errand. [Explore Koh Chang](https://explorekohchang.com/koh-chang/where-to-stay/)

Practical mid-range rooms usually land around **฿1,750–4,300 (US$53–129)**, with December through February at the upper end. Cookies Hotel and Koh Chang Lagoon Resort start from about ฿1,750 in the current local accommodation guide. Chang Buri begins around ฿2,100, while The Erawan begins around ฿3,500. Prices should be checked live before booking, especially over Christmas and New Year. [Explore Koh Chang](https://explorekohchang.com/koh-chang/where-to-stay/)

You can walk to the sand, casual beachfront dining, resort bars, small shops, massages, and the most convenient stretch of services on Koh Chang. The nearest practical transport point is Ao Sapparot ferry pier; a shared songthaew from the pier to White Sand costs around **฿50 (US$1.50)**. There is no metro, BTS, or formal station.

### Klong Prao Beach

Klong Prao is longer, greener, and more spread out. Resorts sit between the beach, coconut-lined lanes, and small canal sections, with more space between properties than at White Sand. It suits a quieter resort stay, couples who want long beach walks, families who do not need late-night activity, and travelers using taxis or scooters to move around. ### Lonely Beach and Bailan

Lonely Beach and nearby Bailan sit farther south on the west coast, where the road grows steeper and the accommodation becomes less polished, more social, and often cheaper. You come here for hillside bungalows, beach bars, dive and tour desks, and an evening scene that is more casual than White Sand. [Explore Koh Chang](https://explorekohchang.com/koh-chang/where-to-stay/)

Budget and simple mid-range doubles typically cost **฿600–2,000 (US$18–60)**, while dorm beds can start around ฿300. Cafe del Sunshine, Slumber Resort, Blue Sky, and Thale are named lower-cost options. Bhumiyama Plus and Siam Beach Resort begin around ฿2,000 in the local 2026 guide. [Explore Koh Chang](https://explorekohchang.com/koh-chang/where-to-stay/)

You can walk to inexpensive food, bars, basic shops, and beach access, though hills and uneven road sections make it less comfortable on foot than White Sand or Kai Bae. Shared taxis from the ferry pier are around **฿100 (US$3)**. Do not count on a late-night songthaew to rescue you after dinner.

## Things to do

### Half-day

**Klong Plu Waterfall** is the most accessible waterfall outing on Koh Chang, set in national-park forest near Klong Prao. You can combine the walk and swim stop with lunch nearby, then return to the beach before the heat peaks. Foreign adult entry is around **฿200 (US$6.01)** and child entry is about ฿100. Go early, particularly in dry season. [Thailand Addict](https://thailandaddict.com/en/koh-chang-guide)

**Than Mayom Waterfall and the east-coast road** makes a quieter counterpoint to the west-coast beach areas. One same-day national-park ticket is reported to cover both Than Mayom and Klong Plu, so the foreign adult cost is again around **฿200 (US$6.01)**. Start in the morning or early afternoon, leaving enough daylight for the return. [Thailand Addict](https://thailandaddict.com/en/koh-chang-guide)

## Local tips + scams

Keep your feet down and low. Do not point with your feet, rest them on tables, aim your soles at Buddha images, or step over people sitting on the floor. It is a small adjustment, but locals notice it.

Take shoes off before entering temples and many private homes. If you are visiting a temple, government office, or someone’s house, cover your shoulders and knees. Beach clothes are for the beach road, not every doorway.

Avoid touching people on the head, including children, unless you know them well. Treat royal imagery carefully too; jokes, gestures, and casual disrespect around the monarchy can create serious problems in Thailand.

Scooter-rental damage disputes remain a practical risk. Video the whole bike before you leave: mirrors, panels, wheels, tires, fuel gauge, existing scratches, and the odometer. Use an established rental shop, ask for a written agreement, and use a cash deposit if possible instead of leaving your passport. Do not drink and ride. Wet surfaces, steep bends, and unlit southern roads are not forgiving. [Baanlyy](https://baanlyy.com/thailand/koh-chang/safety)

Songthaew pricing needs the same clarity. Ask for the fare before you climb in, confirm that it is a shared fare, and ask what happens if no other passengers board. A shared ride can become a private charter without that discussion.

On Koh Rang tours, the quoted tour price may not include the **฿200 foreigner marine-park fee**, which is often collected in cash by rangers on the water. Ask before you pay your deposit, not after the boat has left Bang Bao. [Koh Chang Taxis](https://www.kohchangtaxis.com/guide/koh-chang-snorkeling-island-hopping)

One local habit saves trouble: arrange your return ride before heading south for sunset or dinner. After dark, songthaews are not a reliable hop-on service and app rides are not a dependable backup. For food, stick with properly cooked dishes and avoid raw meat or wildlife dishes when hygiene and storage are unclear.

## FAQ

### Is Koh Chang safe for solo female travelers?

Koh Chang is generally suitable for solo female travelers who use normal island awareness. Book reviewed accommodation, avoid isolated beach stretches late at night, confirm transport fares, watch drinks, and skip scooter riding if you are not licensed and experienced. The practical risks are wet roads, steep bends, mosquitoes, and rental disputes. [Baanlyy](https://baanlyy.com/thailand/koh-chang/safety)

### How many days do I need in Koh Chang?

Plan four or five nights. That allows time for the airport-and-ferry transfer, a beach day, a waterfall or east-coast day, a full snorkeling or diving day, and one weather buffer. In December through February, three nights can work, but monsoon-season itineraries need more flexibility.

### Is Koh Chang expensive?

Koh Chang remains manageable outside the December–February peak. Backpackers can target **US$35–55** daily with dorms or basic bungalows, local food, and shared transport. Mid-range travelers should budget **US$90–159**, while higher-end resorts, diving, private drivers, and festive dates can push spending above US$250 per day. [Explore Koh Chang](https://explorekohchang.com/koh-chang/where-to-stay/) [I Am Koh Chang](https://iamkohchang.com/getting-here/koh-chang-taxi-fares-songtaew.html)

### What is the best area for first-time visitors?

White Sand Beach is the easiest first base because restaurants, shops, tour desks, hotels, and songthaew access sit close together. Kai Bae is a better choice when you prefer a smaller beach village and want to be closer to the southern beaches without staying as far out as Lonely Beach. [Explore Koh Chang](https://explorekohchang.com/koh-chang/where-to-stay/)

### Do I need cash on Koh Chang?

Yes. Resorts and some restaurants accept cards, but cash is still important for songthaews, scooter deposits, small food shops, market purchases, tips, park fees, and boat-tour extras. Carry small baht notes and reserve enough cash for the Koh Rang marine-park fee if your boat enters that zone. [Koh Chang Taxis](https://www.kohchangtaxis.com/guide/koh-chang-snorkeling-island-hopping)

### Can I ride a scooter on Koh Chang?

You can ride legally and more safely if you hold the correct licence and are confident on steep, curving tropical roads. Wear a helmet, inspect and film the bike before leaving, do not drink and ride, and avoid rain if you are not accustomed to wet surfaces. Use a songthaew or arranged driver when unsure. [Baanlyy](https://baanlyy.com/thailand/koh-chang/safety)
